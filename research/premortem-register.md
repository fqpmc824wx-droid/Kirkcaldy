# Kirkcaldy Dental: merged launch risk register (8 Nov 2026)

No plan can make a launch risk-free. In this register, "bulletproof" means three things:
- Nothing important can fail without someone being told quickly.
- Every alert reaches a person who acts on it.
- Every failure has a written fix.

Rows marked **gap** or **partly** are where that isn't true yet. The gap list at the end is ordered by deadline.

- **What each status is based on:**
  - committed code on `main` (5da6217, 21:16) and on `claude/brave-allen-i14xnx` (98823e2, 21:18);
  - the runbook, HANDOFF, BRAND and ads/CREATIVE-REVIEW.md.
- **Work in progress, not counted as covered:** the ads-chat working tree has uncommitted edits to `leads/meta-leads.gs`:
  - a META_API_VERSION property defaulting to v27.0;
  - duplicate handling for Meta leads;

  - a mail-quota guard;

  These count only once they're on `main` and pasted into Apps Script.
- **Scoring:** likelihood High/Medium/Low = 3/2/1; impact Severe/Major/Minor = 3/2/1; score = L×I. Groups are ordered by their worst risk, and rows within each group by score.
