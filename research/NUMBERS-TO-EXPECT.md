# Numbers to expect and what to watch

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic waiting list · 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW · written 8 Nov 2026 (updated the same day: callers are not a limit)

These are best guesses until the ads have run for 3–4 days. After that, we use the real numbers.

- About half of the people on the list are likely to book.
- **Why the forecast is lower than £5:** it's a free NHS waiting list, there are very few NHS places locally, and the form is quick and filled in for them. Private dental ads cost far more per lead. The warnings below are set to your £5 to be safe.
- The form only asks for name, phone and (optional) email. Household size and postcode come from the call, so "people on the list" grows as calls are made.

## 2. The 6 numbers to glance at every morning

Look in Ads Manager, the 9pm email and the Waiting List sheet. (The Dashboard tab in the sheet is still being built.)

| # | Number | 🟢 Green: carry on | 🟠 Amber: keep an eye on it | 🔴 Red: do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cost per sign-up**, last 3 days (Ads Manager) | Under £4 | £4–£5 | Over £5 for 3 days: fix the ad or form, don't add money, tell the ads chat. Over £8: pause and ask the ads chat. |
| 2 | **Sign-ups yesterday**, all sources (sheet) | 10–20 a day | More than 20 a day per caller on shift | Over 20 a day per caller for 3 days: add a caller. Fewer than 3 Facebook sign-ups a day for 3 days: check the ads are running and the form works. |
| 3 | **Leads waiting for a call** (9pm email) | Everyone called within an hour | Some waiting 1–4 hours | The backlog email arrives, or anyone has waited over 24 hours: add a caller the same day. Only if nobody is available, cut the budget by 20%. |
| 4 | **Are the ads running?** (Ads Manager) | Spent close to the daily budget, no rejected ads, card OK | Spent under half the budget for 1 day | Nothing spent by midday, an ad rejected, or a payment problem: tell the ads chat the same day. |
| 5 | **How often people see the ad** ("Frequency", last 7 days) | Under 2 | 2–3 | Over 3: ask the ads chat for 1–2 fresh versions of the best ad. |
| 6 | **Calls that reach the person**, within 3 tries (sheet Status) | 7 or more in 10 | 6 in 10 | Under 6 in 10: try calling at different times, and tell the ads chat (it may be poor-quality leads). |

**Week 1: change nothing for 3–4 days**, even if the numbers look odd. Meta is still learning.

**When to raise the budget:** when cost per sign-up is under £4 for 3 days and there is no backlog email. Raise by 20%, no more than once every 3–4 days. Make sure there are enough callers on shift first.

**Black Friday (27 Nov) and Christmas** push prices up 15–30%. That is normal. Don't panic.

## 3. How many callers you need

Calling is not a limit: you have staff available. The budget goes up or down on **cost per sign-up only**.

Rule of thumb: **one caller for every 20–25 new sign-ups a day.**

| Daily budget (Ads Manager) | Likely sign-ups a day, all sources | Callers on shift |
|---|---|---|
| £20 | about 22 | 1 |
| £25 | about 30 | 2 |
| £35 | about 37 | 2 |
| £50 | about 46 | 2–3 |

- **Before each budget step**, check there are enough callers on shift for the extra sign-ups.
- **Add a caller the same day** if any of these happens:
  - sign-ups stay above 20 a day per caller;
  - the backlog email arrives;
  - a newspaper story or busy local post lands.
- The aim is that **every new sign-up gets a call within an hour** (between 8am and 9pm).

## 4. The true cost

Ads Manager shows the budget **before** extras. Meta adds a **2% UK fee** and then **20% VAT**. The clinic can't claim the VAT back, because dental care is VAT-exempt. So every £1 in Ads Manager really costs about **£1.22**.

| Ads Manager says | You really pay a day | You really pay a week |
|---|---|---|
| £20 a day | **£24.48** | about £171 |
| £25 a day | £30.60 | about £214 |
| £35 a day | £42.84 | about £300 |
| £50 a day | £61.20 | about £428 |

- **4-week plan:** about £810 in Ads Manager, so **about £990 really paid**.
- **Cost per sign-up:** about £1.50 in Ads Manager, so **about £1.84 really paid** (range about 80p to £4.90). If it is £5, you really pay about £6.12.
- **Cost per household that books:** about £3.40 really paid.
- **Texts:** add about 9p per sign-up.
- **Safety cap:** set the Ads Manager **account spending limit to about £900** (Billing → Payment settings). The 4-week plan is about £810, so this stops any mistake or hacked account from overspending. You'll get an email before it runs out.

## 6. Every Monday (about 30 minutes, one job)

Ask the Metrics chat **"How are we doing?"**. You get one report with:
- sign-ups, cost per sign-up and spend, compared with this forecast;
- **one clear budget decision** for the week (hold, up 20%, or down 20%), using the rules above;
- a short checklist for you to tick: Ads Manager activity (no strangers or unknown changes), card and spending-limit headroom, who has access to the Page and ad account, ClickSend balance, 5 calls listened to and 10 rows spot-checked, complaints due, backups, and the calendar dates above.

The Metrics chat never changes the ads, the budget or the sheet itself.
