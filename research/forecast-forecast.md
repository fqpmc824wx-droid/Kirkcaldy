**Short answer**
- **Cost per lead:** expect about **£1.50** (about £1.80 if Meta adds VAT). The £20–£80 figures you hear are for private implant and Invisalign ads, where people are being asked to pay. They do not apply to a free NHS place.
- **Ads alone:** £20 a day should bring about **15 sign-ups a day, or about 100 a week**. The full £20→£50 plan should bring about **540 Meta leads in 4 weeks for about £810**.
- **"A lot?"** From ads alone before opening, you get a steady stream, not a flood. From all channels together, yes: about **1,650 sign-ups (~3,150 people) by opening** and **~1,700 new registrations in the opening month**.
- **£20 is enough.** Your one caller will run out before the budget does.
- **Glenrothes numbers:** the 1,187 (Feb) and 2,598 (Mar) are registrations *after opening*, from every channel, with unknown ad spend. They show the demand is real. They cannot tell us what each £1 of ads will buy.

## 1) Expected cost per lead

These are Ads Manager figures before VAT. If the clinic has no UK VAT number, Meta adds 20% VAT (runbook section 8).

| Case | Cost per lead | With VAT | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | ~£0.65 | ~£0.80 | The ad is clearly NHS, it is shared widely in local groups, and form completion is high |
| **Likely** | **£1.35 in week 1, ~£1.30 in week 2, ~£1.50 in week 3, ~£1.75 by early Dec (~£1.50 over the 4 weeks)** | **~£1.80** | A normal launch |
| High | £3.50–£4.40 | £4.20–£5.30 | Weak ad response or a form people abandon |
| Separate risk: the ad looks like a private clinic | ~£4.40 (£2.60–£7.50) | ~£5.30 | "Dental & Implant Clinic" reads as private if "NHS" is not obvious |

**Why the cost should be this low**
- All three forecasts use similar view costs (CPM ~£8–9). The difference from private-dental ads is the response, not the price of the clicks. A free NHS place in a town where 1 of 57 practices is accepting patients (2026 aggregator) turns about 0.6–0.9% of ad views into a lead. Private dental ads manage about 0.06–0.1%.
- Kirkcaldy access is getting worse. NHS registrations at KY1/KY2 practices fell by 3,511 (-6.2%) between March 2025 and March 2026 (PHS open data).
- People still travel 6–10 miles from Kirkcaldy postcodes to the Leven and Glenrothes practices: about 130–200 a month even now.

**Why the cost will drift up**
- Week 1 costs 10–35% more while Meta's learning phase runs.
- Black Friday (27 Nov) and Cyber Monday (30 Nov) push view costs up 15–30%, rising again into December.
- Frequency rises in a small area served by only two ads.

**What a booking costs:** £1.50 ÷ 0.55 ≈ **£2.75 per booked household** (≈£3.30 with VAT), or about £1.50 per person.

## 4) Will one caller cope?

**What one caller can actually handle**
- 30–40 calls a day is not 30–40 leads. No-answers and 8am call-backs mean about 1.6–1.7 calls per lead.
- So one person can properly work about **20–25 new leads a day**.
- The runbook's "~30–40 leads/day for one person" (section 8) is too generous.

Daily sign-ups from all sources, likely case:

| Week | Meta + shares | Website, groups, press, QR, leaflets | Total a day | One caller |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (£20) | ~17 | ~5 | ~22 | At capacity |
| 2 (£20–25) | ~21 | ~9 | ~30 | About a third over |
| 3 (£25–35) | ~25 | ~12 | ~37 | About half over |
| 4 (£35–50) | ~31 | ~15 | ~46 | Nearly double |

**Verdict.** One caller copes with week 1 at £20, and only if organic sign-ups are slow. **A second caller is needed from week 2 (around 17–20 Nov), and before the budget goes above £25 a day.** Over 6 weeks one person can work about 850–1,000 leads, against about 1,650 expected, so roughly half the list.

**Bring the second caller in when any of these happens**
- Sign-ups from all sources stay above 20 a day for 3 days running.
- The backlog warning (15+ leads waiting over an hour) fires on 2 evenings in a week.
- The 9pm report shows leads waiting more than 24 hours.
- A press story or viral post lands. Add the caller the same day.

**If there is no second caller**
- Hold Meta at £20, or cut it by 20% as the runbook says.
- Add a self-booking link to the instant SMS and email.
- Call the newest leads first, so fresh leads are reached quickly while older ones already have the confirmation text.
- Speed matters: leads left more than 3 days book at about 0.45 instead of 0.56.

With two callers (about 40–50 leads a day), the £35–£50 steps fit.

## 5) Is £20 a day enough?

**Yes.** £20 a day is enough to start, and for one caller it is close to the right ceiling.
- It brings about 15 leads a day and about 100 a week.
- That clears Meta's ~50-a-week learning threshold in week 1.
- Even at a bad £3.50 a lead it still brings about 6 a day.

£10 a day also works, at about 7 a day, but it is slower and the learning phase is shakier. The budget is not the limit. The caller is.

**Rules for changing the budget**
1. Change nothing for the first 3–4 days, then judge on real numbers after £60–100 of spend.
2. Step up by about 20–25% only if both of these hold:
   - Cost per lead over the last 3 days is under £2.50.
   - The backlog warning is not firing.
3. Do not go above £25 a day until a second caller or a self-booking link is in place.
4. If cost per lead is over £3.50 for 3 days, fix the ad or the form; do not add money. Over £5, pause and rebuild. **Lower the runbook's £6 alarm to £3.50.**

## 6) What would make leads expensive, and the warning signs

**Causes, most likely first**
1. **The ad does not read as NHS.** "Dental & Implant Clinic" sounds private. That scenario costs about £4.40 a lead and brings 4–5 a day. Fix: put "NHS" in the first line, in the headline and on the image.
2. **"Opening soon / waiting list" wording** is weaker than "registering now". It is the honest wording, so make the scarcity and the location clear instead.
3. **Form friction.** The Higher-intent form has a review screen plus postcode and 3 multiple-choice questions. Completion under 25% means people are giving up.
4. **Q4 ad prices.** Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Christmas add 15–30%. This is expected and is not a reason to panic.
5. **Fatigue.** Only 2 ads run to about 95–135k Meta users. Frequency above about 3 pushes costs up.
6. **Scaling too fast.** Big budget jumps restart Meta's learning phase. Keep to steps of 20–25%.
7. **Placements bringing junk.** Advantage+ placements can bring cheap, junk Audience Network leads. The cost looks fine but they don't answer the phone.
8. **New Page with no social proof**, which hurts week 1.

**Confidence**
