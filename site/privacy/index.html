Privacy, Complaints & Regulation

Last updated: November 2026

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use your personal information when you join our waiting list, contact us, or use this website.

Who we are

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic is a trading name of Lang Toun Smiles Ltd, a company registered in Scotland (company number SC887824). Registered office: 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW.
Email: info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk
We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

What information we collect

When you join our waiting list, we collect:

  • Your full name, phone number and email address
  • Your postcode, if you give it
  • How many people you would like to add to the waiting list, if you tell us
  • Whether you asked to receive news and offers
  • When you signed up and how you heard about us

We do not ask for medical information at this stage.

How we use your information

  • To add you to our waiting list and confirm we have your details by text and email
  • To contact you about your waiting-list request
  • To answer any questions you send us

We only send news or offers to people who ask for them. We never sell your information.

You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time: email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk, or reply STOP to any text.

Our lawful basis

We contact you about your waiting-list request because you asked us to (legitimate interests). We only send news and offers with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data only on our instructions: our website host, our email and document storage provider, our text-message provider, and Meta (for sign-ups made through Facebook or Instagram). Where any provider processes data outside the UK, appropriate legal safeguards are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep your waiting-list details for up to 12 months, or until you ask us to remove them. If you become a patient, your records are kept in line with professional record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. To remove your details or make a request, email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk.

Data protection complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, please tell us first: email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk or write to us at 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and let you know what we are doing about it.

If you are still unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

NHS & private care

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic is a new NHS dental practice that will also offer private treatments. Full details of NHS and private services and fees will be published before we open.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with any aspect of our service, please contact the Practice Manager at info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk.

For NHS treatment, you can also contact the NHS Fife Patient Experience Team or the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman. For private treatment, you can contact the Dental Complaints Service.

Regulation

All dental professionals working at the practice are registered with the General Dental Council (GDC). You can check a dental professional's registration at gdc-uk.org.
