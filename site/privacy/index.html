Privacy, Complaints & Regulation

Last updated: November 2026

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use your personal information when you join our waiting list, contact us, or use this website.

Who we are

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic is a trading name of Lang Toun Smiles Ltd, a company registered in Scotland (company number SC887824). Registered office: 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW.
Email: info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk
We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

What information we collect

When you join our waiting list on this website or through our Facebook or Instagram form, we collect:

  • Your full name, phone number and email address
  • Your postcode, if you give it
  • How many people you would like to add to the waiting list, if you tell us
  • Whether you ticked the optional box to receive news and offers
  • The date and time you signed up, and where you found us (for example a leaflet, our banner or a Facebook ad)

We do not ask for medical information at this stage.

How we use your information

  • To add you to our waiting list and confirm we have your details by text and email
  • To text and call you about your waiting-list request, between 8am and 9pm
  • To answer any questions you send us

We only send news or offers to people who ticked the optional box to receive them. We never sell your information.

You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time: email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk, reply STOP to any text, or tell us when we call.

Our lawful basis

We contact you about your waiting-list request because you asked us to add you to the list (our legitimate interest in responding to your request). We only send news and offers if you gave your consent by ticking the optional box, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to manage these sign-ups, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram), which hosts our Facebook and Instagram sign-up form
  • Google (Google Workspace: Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail), which stores sign-ups and sends emails
  • ClickSend, which sends our text messages (ClickSend is based in Australia; transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum)
  • Hostinger, which hosts this website

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens, they use appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your waiting-list details for 12 months from when you sign up, or until you ask us to remove them. If we have not opened by then, we will ask whether you want to stay on the list; if you do, we keep your details for another 12 months. If you later become a patient, your records are kept in line with professional record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. To remove your details or make a request, email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk.

Data protection complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, please tell us first: email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk or write to us at 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and let you know what we are doing about it.

If you are still unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record your IP address.

NHS & private care

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic is a new NHS dental practice that will also offer private treatments. Full details of NHS and private services and fees will be published before we open.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with any aspect of our service, please contact the Practice Manager at info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk.

For NHS treatment, you can also contact the NHS Fife Patient Experience Team or the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman. For private treatment, you can contact the Dental Complaints Service.

Regulation

All dental professionals working at the practice are registered with the General Dental Council (GDC). You can check a dental professional's registration at gdc-uk.org.
