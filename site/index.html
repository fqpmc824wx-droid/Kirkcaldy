Patient information

NHS & private care

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic is a new NHS dental practice that will also offer private treatments. Full details of NHS and private services and fees will be published before we open.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with any aspect of our service, please contact the Practice Manager on [PHONE] or at info@kirkcaldydentist.co.uk.

For NHS treatment, you can also contact NHS Fife Patient Experience Team or the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman. For private treatment, you can contact the Dental Complaints Service.

Regulation

All dental professionals working at the practice are registered with the General Dental Council (GDC). You can check a dental professional's registration at gdc-uk.org.
