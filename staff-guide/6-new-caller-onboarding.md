# 6. New caller onboarding

*Draft, 8 Nov 2026. About 1 hour. Do all of this before your first real call.*

The owner says callers are not a limit. We add callers as sign-ups grow: about **one caller for every 20 to 25 new sign-ups a day**. With 2 or more callers, sign-ups take turns, so each person is sent to just one caller.

## 1. Read (30 minutes)

1. **Page 0, the call card.** Keep it by the phone.
2. **Page 1, the caller script.** All of it.
3. **Page 2, message templates.** Just M1 (missed-call text) for now.
4. **Page 8, if things break.** Just "Texts have stopped" and "I can't open the sheet".

## 2. Get set up (with the practice manager)

- [ ] Your **own** Google login for the Waiting List sheet. Never share a login.
- [ ] Two-step sign-in turned on for that login.
- [ ] Edit access to the sheet, and the "To call" filter view.
- [ ] The practice phone app on your **work** phone, with the practice number showing.
- [ ] Your work mobile number added to the alert list by the owner or deputy.
- [ ] A test: the manager sends a test sign-up, you get the alert, you find the row, then the manager deletes it.

## 3. Practise (15 minutes)

- Do 2 practice calls with the manager playing the patient: one normal, one where the "patient" is in pain and wants to be seen today.
- Fill in a practice row: Postcode, No. of People, Adult/Child/Family, Current practice, Call outcome.

## 4. Data protection basics

- The sheet holds people's names, numbers and sometimes health details. Treat it like a patient record.
- Only open it to do your job. Never copy, download, print or screenshot it.
- **Alert texts** (a name and a phone number) only go to your **work** phone. **Delete each alert after you've called the person**, and check every Friday that none are left.
- Notes are facts only. Never health details. Pain = "signposted [date]" only.
- Children: names and dates of birth only, given by the parent.
- Never talk about one person's details with anyone else, even family members, unless they're on the same sign-up.
- Lock your screen and phone when you step away.
- Lost phone, wrong person sent a message, or anything else that feels like a data slip: tell the practice manager **straight away**. We may have to report it within 72 hours.
- "Stop" means stop: DO NOT CONTACT the same day.

## 5. Your first week

- The owner listens to some of your calls (5 a week across all callers for the first 4 weeks). That's normal. It helps us all say the same thing.
- If you're ill or can't work, tell the owner **and** the backup caller **by 7:30am**.
- Questions you can't answer: "Let me check and call you back." Then ask the practice manager.

## 6. Confidentiality note (sign before your first call)

I'll delete alert texts after calling. I'll report any slip straight away. This still applies after I stop working here.
