# 9. Black Friday and Christmas plan

*Draft, 8 Nov 2026. The owner fills in the cover table **by 1 December**.*

## The plan

- **25 Nov to 2 Dec (Black Friday week):** no ad budget rises. Ads cost more that week.
- **By 1 Dec:** the owner names who covers every day from 24 Dec to 4 Jan (table below). If a day has no caller, the ads drop to £5-10 a day or pause for that time.
- **By 23 Dec:** nobody left waiting for a first call.
- **Bank holidays in Scotland:** Fri 25 Dec, Mon 28 Dec (for Boxing Day), Fri 1 Jan, Mon 4 Jan (for 2 Jan).
- Calls are still only 8am to 9pm, on any day we're working.
