# 10. Briefing for reception at our other practices

*Draft, 9 Nov 2026. One page for front-desk staff at the group's other Fife practices. People may ask you about the new Kirkcaldy practice.*

## The facts

- **Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic** is a new NHS dental practice **opening soon**, offering NHS and private dental care, at **23-25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW**.
- People join a **waiting list** at **kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register** or through our Facebook and Instagram ads.
- Joining is free. We never ask for payment to join or for the first exam.
- We have **no opening date** to share yet.

## What to say

**"Can I get on the Kirkcaldy list?"**

> "Yes, please join online at kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register. The team will call you to take your details."

Don't write their details down yourself, and don't add them to any list. The online form is the only way in.

**"I'm registered here. Should I move to Kirkcaldy?"**

> "That's up to you. In Scotland, registering with a new practice ends your registration here. But joining the Kirkcaldy waiting list doesn't change anything. Your registration here only ends if you're invited and go to your first appointment there."

Never push anyone to move, and never put anyone off.

**"When does it open? How long is the wait?"**

> "There's no date yet. Everyone on the list gets updates. They invite people in stages as appointments become available."

**"Can you move me up the list?"** No. Nobody can. We don't treat anyone differently because they're a patient here, and paying for private care never changes a place.

**"I'm in pain."** Treat them as you normally would if they're your patient. If they're not your patient: "Please contact your own dentist, or phone NHS 24 on 111."

## Never say

"accepting" or "taking on" patients, "first come, first served", "limited places", "guaranteed", "free NHS dentist", or any date.

## Questions you can't answer

Ask them to email **info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk**.
