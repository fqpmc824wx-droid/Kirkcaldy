# 3. Facebook and Instagram: comment and message replies

*Draft, 8 Nov 2026. Not for use until the owner approves it.*

## Rules

- Reply within a few hours, 8am to 9pm.
- Say "waiting list". Never say "accepting" or "taking on" patients. Never say "first come, first served". Never promise a date or a place.
- Keep replies admin only. Don't push private treatment or offers.
- **Never confirm in public that a person is on our list.** Move it to a private message.
- **Hide** (don't delete) any comment with a phone number, address, email, or health details (like "my tooth is broken and I'm on antibiotics"). Then reply in public: "Thanks, we've hidden your comment to protect your privacy. We'll send you a message." Then message them privately.
- If someone says "stop" or "remove me", set Status to **DO NOT CONTACT** in the sheet the same day.
- Rude comments: don't argue. Reply once, politely, or hide it if it's abusive. Tell the practice manager.
- The people in our ads are stock or computer-made images. Never say they are our patients or staff.
- If you're not sure, don't guess. Ask the practice manager.

## The 10 most likely questions

### 1. "Are you taking NHS patients?"

> Thanks for asking! We're a new NHS dental practice opening soon at 23-25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy. Please join our waiting list at kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register and we'll be in touch.

### 2. "When do you open?"

> We don't have a confirmed opening date yet. If you join our waiting list at kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register, we'll send you updates as soon as we know.

### 3. "How do I join? Does it cost anything?"

> Joining is free. Tap Sign up on our post or go to kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register. We'll text and email to confirm, then call you to check your details.

### 4. "I'm already with another dentist. Can I still join?"

> Yes, you can join. Joining isn't the same as registering. In Scotland, registering with a new practice ends your old registration, but nothing changes until we invite you and you've had your first appointment with us.

### 5. "Can I add my family / kids?"

> Yes. Adults, children and families can all join. Put everyone in your household on one sign-up and we'll keep you together.

### 6. "I'm in pain. Can you see me?"
*Hide the comment if it has health details. Then send a private message:*

> Sorry to hear you're in pain. We're not open yet, so we can't see anyone for urgent problems. Please contact your current dentist, or phone NHS 24 on 111. You're welcome to join our waiting list too.

### 7. "I signed up. Where am I on the list? / Nobody has called me."
*Reply in public, then check the sheet and message privately:*

> Thanks for joining! We'll send you a private message to check.

Private message:

> Thanks for your patience. We're calling everyone who joined, 8am to 9pm. We invite people in stages as appointments become available, and we can't give dates yet. We'll keep you updated.

### 8. "How long is the wait?"

> We can't give a date yet. We'll invite people in stages as appointments become available, keeping households together. Everyone on the list gets regular updates.

### 9. "Do you do implants / private treatment?"

> Yes, we'll offer NHS and private dental care, including implants. Private treatment is completely separate from the NHS waiting list and never affects your place on it.

### 10. "Is this real? Do I have to pay?"

> Yes, we're real: Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, 23-25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW. Joining is free. We never ask for payment or bank details to join or for your first exam.

## Other situations

- **"Can I just walk in to sign up?"** "Please don't queue. Join online at kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register and we'll contact you."
- **"What does NHS treatment cost?"** "NHS exams are free in Scotland. Some treatment has a charge, depending on your age and situation. We'll explain everything before any treatment." *(The manager checks current charges before any figures are given.)*
- **"Why did my friend get called first?"** "We contact everyone, and we invite people in stages. Please send us a private message and we'll check your details."

## Set up once on the Facebook Page (blocked words and away message)

**Blocked words** (Page settings → Moderation). Comments with these are hidden automatically. A person then checks them and replies privately:

- phone-number starts: 07, +44, 01592
- @ (catches email addresses)
- abscess, swelling, swollen, antibiotics, bleeding, infection, broken tooth, toothache, pregnant
- scam, fraud
- swear words (use Facebook's "profanity filter" on "strong")

Check the hidden comments twice a day (page 7). Someone in pain still gets the pain reply (question 6) by private message.

**Away message** (Inbox → Automations → Away message, on outside 8am to 9pm):

> Thanks for your message! Our team replies between 8am and 9pm. To join our waiting list, go to kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register. If you're in pain, please contact your current dentist or phone NHS 24 on 111.

**Instant reply** (first message from someone new, any time):

> Thanks for getting in touch with Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic. We're a new NHS dental practice opening soon. Join our waiting list at kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register. We'll reply here soon.
