# 0. Call card (keep by the phone)

*Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic. Draft, 8 Nov 2026. **The owner approves this card before the first call.** For the first 4 weeks the owner listens to 5 calls a week.*

## Times
Call **8am to 9pm only**. Start your last call by **8:45pm**. Order: Call 8am rows, then blank rows (oldest first), then call-backs due.

## Every call: say these

1. **Your name**, "**Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic**", and why: "You joined our waiting list."
2. "We're a **new NHS dental practice opening soon** on Tolbooth Street."
3. "Joining the list **isn't registration**."
4. "In Scotland, registering with a new practice **ends your old registration**. But **nothing changes until we invite you** and you come to your first appointment."
5. "We **never ask for payment** to join or for your first exam. Never bank details."

## Every call: ask these

- "Who else in your home would like to join?" (names; children's dates of birth, given by the parent)
- "What's your postcode?"
- "**Where is your current dentist?**"
- "How did you hear about us?"
- "Is anyone in pain now?"

## Never say

- "accepting" or "taking on" patients
- "first come, first served", "limited places", "guaranteed", "free NHS dentist"
- any date, or a promise of a place
- anything selling private treatment (only answer if they ask: "It's separate and never affects your NHS waiting list place.")
- that the people in our ads are our patients

## Pain or urgent

> "Please contact your current dentist. If you don't have one, or it's evenings or weekends, phone **NHS 24 on 111**."

- No dentist, working hours: NHS Fife dental line **[NHS FIFE DENTAL LINE - manager to check]**.
- Bleeding that won't stop, or swelling affecting breathing or swallowing: **999**.
- Pain never moves anyone up the list.
- Notes: write only **"signposted [date]"**. Facts only, never health details. Tell the manager today.

## "Stop" means stop
Anyone who says stop, by any route: pick **DO NOT CONTACT** the **same day**. Never contact them again.

## After every call
Pick the **Call outcome** (column Y). Fill in Postcode, No. of People, Adult/Child/Family, Current practice, How heard, Assigned to.

## Not sure?
Don't guess. Say "Let me check and call you back," and ask the practice manager.
