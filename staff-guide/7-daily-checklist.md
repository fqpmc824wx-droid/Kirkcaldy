# 7. Daily checklist (about 15 minutes)

*Draft, 8 Nov 2026. Done by the staff checker. At weekends: the named weekend cover, or the owner.*

## 8:00am (10 minutes)

1. **Ads Manager:**
   - the campaign is On and Active;
   - both ads are Active. Nothing says Rejected or "With issues";
   - yesterday's spend was close to the budget;
   - no billing warning at the top.
   - If the campaign switched itself off (the 30-leads rule), turn it back on **only after** the caller says the overnight sign-ups are called.
   - Any problem: see page 8 and tell the owner.
2. **business.facebook.com/accountquality:** no warning on the Page or the ad account.
3. **Waiting List sheet, "To call" view:**
   - How many rows say "Call 8am"? Tell the caller(s).
   - Is the caller logging calls since their start time?
   - No caller on? Tell the owner straight away (page 8, "The caller is off").
4. **Health log tab:** any problem still open? Make sure the owner knows.
5. **Facebook and Instagram inbox** (comments and messages since last night):
   - reply using page 3;
   - hide comments with phone numbers, addresses or health details, and send a private message instead;
   - "stop" → caller picks DO NOT CONTACT today;
   - pain → the pain reply on page 3;
   - "nobody called me" → find their row and tell the caller;
   - a fake page or scam → tell the owner.

## 8:30pm (5 minutes)

6. Check the inbox again. Note how many are still unanswered.
7. Fill in today's row in the **Daily checks** tab: comments new / replied / unanswered at 9pm, risky comments, messages, typical reply time, most common question, caller hours, who worked.

## Mondays, also

- Search Facebook and Instagram for "Kirkcaldy Dental" copycat pages. Report any to the owner.
- Scan the banner's QR code with your phone. Check it opens the sign-up page.

## Weekend version (about 15 minutes)

Same as above, with these changes:

- **8am:** steps 1 to 5 as normal. If no caller is booked for the weekend, tell the owner by 8:30am. The owner decides whether to cut the ad budget.
- **Midday (2 minutes):** quick look at the inbox. Hide anything with personal or health details.
- **8:30pm:** steps 6 and 7 as normal.
- Saturday and Sunday calls are 8am to 9pm, unless the owner picks a 9am start.
- If anything is broken and you can't reach the owner, call the **deputy** (names on page 5).
