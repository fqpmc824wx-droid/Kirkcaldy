# 8. If things break: what staff do

*Draft, 8 Nov 2026. This page is for callers and the staff checker. The technical fixes are in the launch risk plan (runbooks R1 to R10). The owner or deputy does those. Your job: keep calling, tell the right person, and don't touch the settings.*

## Golden rules

- **Don't change any settings**, scripts or keys. Don't delete rows. Don't create new Facebook pages or ad accounts.
- **Tell the owner first, then the deputy** (names in the Launch safety plan, see page 5). Say what you saw and when.
- **Keep calling.** People still need their call even when the system has a problem.

## Manual mode (when texts or alerts aren't working)

If texts or caller alerts stop, callers work straight from the sheet:

1. Open the Waiting List sheet, "To call" view.
2. Call in this order: **Call 8am** rows, then **blank Status** rows, oldest first, then call-backs due.
3. Check the **Meta Business Suite app** on your work phone. New Facebook sign-ups still pop up there.
4. Do the calls as normal. Pick a Call outcome after each one.
5. Don't send texts from your own phone. Don't send bulk texts from the ClickSend website.
6. Note in Notes: "No auto text - manual mode [date]".

## Texts have stopped

*Signs: people say they didn't get a text; the SMS status column says Failed; an alert email says ClickSend credit is low.*

1. Tell the owner or deputy. (They follow R4.)
2. Switch to **manual mode** (above).
3. When it's fixed, the owner or deputy sends any missed thank-you texts. Not you.

## I can't open the sheet, or it looks wrong

*Signs: "You need access"; rows have gone; columns have moved; strange words in Status.*

1. **Don't try to fix it.** Don't undo, sort or delete.
2. Tell the owner or deputy straight away. Say what you see.
3. Meanwhile, use the **Meta Business Suite app** for new Facebook sign-ups. Write each person's name, number and the time you called on paper. Keep the paper safe and shred it once the details are in the sheet.
4. Only call people you were alerted to. Don't guess.

## An ad is rejected or switched off

*Signs: Ads Manager says Rejected, "With issues", or a billing warning.*

1. **Don't edit the ad.** Don't make a new ad, page or account.
2. Take a screenshot of the message.
3. Send it to the owner. (They follow R1, R2 or R10 with the ads chat.)
4. The other ad, the leaflets and the website keep bringing sign-ups. Keep calling as normal.

## The caller is off

1. The caller tells the owner **and** the backup caller **by 7:30am**.
2. The owner or deputy switches the alerts to the backup. (R5.)
3. The backup works from the "To call" view: Call 8am first, then blanks, oldest first.
4. No backup? The owner cuts or pauses the ads. People still get their automatic text.

## Too many people waiting

*Signs: more than 10 people waiting, or someone waiting over a day.*

1. Tell the owner the same hour. The owner cuts the ad budget or adds a caller. (R6.)
2. Call order: Call 8am, then blanks oldest first, then call-backs due.
3. Anyone not reached after 3 days gets one sorry text from the owner or deputy.

## Website sign-ups have stopped

*Signs: no website or leaflet rows for a day; someone says the page didn't work.*

1. Tell the owner. (R7.)
2. Tell people on Facebook to use the Sign up button on our post instead.

## Lots of strange sign-ups (spam)

*Signs: many rows in a few minutes, made-up names.*

1. Don't call them. Tell the owner. (R8.)
2. If a real person says "I never signed up", say sorry, pick DO NOT CONTACT, and tell the owner.

## A complaint

1. Listen. Say sorry for how they feel. Don't argue.
2. Ask them to email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk so we can look into it properly.
3. Tell the practice manager the same day. (R9.)
4. In public comments, never post their details.
