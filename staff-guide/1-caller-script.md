# 1. Caller script

*Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, 23-25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW. Draft, 8 Nov 2026. Not for use until the owner approves it. The one-page call card (page 0) is the short version to keep by the phone.*

## Before you start

- You call people who joined our waiting list. They asked us to call. Be friendly and clear.
- Calls are **8am to 9pm only**. Start your last call by **8:45pm**. Never call after 9pm.
- People who join after 9pm show **Call 8am** in the sheet. Call them first thing the next morning.
- **Call order:** Call 8am rows first, then blank rows (oldest first), then call-backs that are due (**Next call due**).
- **Each new sign-up is sent to one caller.** If there are 2 or more of you, sign-ups take turns. Only call the ones sent to you. Put your name in **Assigned to** before you dial, so nobody else rings the same person.
- Call from the practice phone or app, not your own mobile.
- Rows marked **Duplicate** are repeat sign-ups. Don't call them. Check the earlier row named in Notes.
- Never sell private treatment on these calls. Only answer questions if the person asks.

### Words we never use

Never say "accepting" or "taking on" patients. Never say "first come, first served", "limited places", "guaranteed" or "free NHS dentist". Never give a date we can't promise.

Say: "We're a **new NHS dental practice opening soon**", "join our waiting list" and "we'll invite you to book".

## The first call (about 3 minutes)

The sign-up form only asks for a name, phone and email. **Ask every person these, every time:** who's in the household (how many, adults or children), the postcode, where their current dentist is, and how they heard about us.

**1. Say who you are and why you're calling.**

> "Hi, is that [first name]? It's [your name] from Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic. We're a new NHS dental practice opening soon on Tolbooth Street. You joined our waiting list, so I'm calling to take a few details. Have you got two minutes?"

**2. Who's in the household?**

> "Is it just you, or would anyone else in your home like to join? Can I take their names? And for any children, their dates of birth?"

- For children under 16, the parent or guardian gives the details. Note which parent is applying. Names and dates of birth only.
- Remind parents that babies can join too.

**3. Postcode.**

> "What's your postcode?"

Read it back to check.

**4. Current dentist.**

> "Where is your current dentist?"

Then say:

> "Just so you know: joining our list isn't the same as registering. In Scotland, when you register with a new practice, your registration at your old one ends. But nothing changes until we invite you and you come to your first appointment. So you won't be left without a dentist while you wait."

**5. How they heard.**

> "How did you hear about us?" (Facebook, Instagram, leaflet, banner, friend, other)

**6. How it works.**

> "We invite people from the list in stages as appointments become available, and we keep households together. When it's your turn, we'll text, email or phone you. I can't give you a date yet, but we'll keep you updated."

**7. The money line.** Say this on every call:

> "We never ask for payment to join the list or for your first exam. We never ask for bank details. If anyone does, it isn't us."

**8. Pain check.**

> "Is anyone in pain now?"

If yes, see **Pain or urgent problems** below.

**9. Check their details and close.**
Check the spelling of their name and their email.

> "Is there anything you'd like to ask? Thanks, [first name]. We'll be in touch."

Then fill in the sheet straight away (see below).

## How to type the details into the sheet

| Column | What to type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Postcode | Full postcode, capitals, with the space | KY1 1RW |
| No. of People | Total number joining, including them, as a number | 3 |
| Adult/Child/Family | **Adult** (adults only), **Child** (only children), or **Family** (adults and children) | Family |
| Currently Registered? | **Yes**, **No** or **Not sure** | Yes |
| Current practice | Practice name and town, or "None" | Bridge St Dental, Kirkcaldy |
| How heard | Facebook, Instagram, Leaflet, Banner, Friend or Other | Leaflet |
| Notes | Facts only: names of everyone else; children's dates of birth; which parent applies | Partner Sam; Ava 03/05/2020 (mum applying) |
| Assigned to | Your name | Jo |

**Notes are facts only. Never put health details in Notes** (no "broken tooth", no medicines). If someone was in pain, write only "signposted [date]".

## Pain or urgent problems

We can't see people on the waiting list for urgent problems yet. Being in pain does **not** move anyone up the list.

Say:

> "I'm sorry you're in pain. Please contact your current dentist first. If you don't have one, or it's evening or the weekend, phone NHS 24 on 111."

- In working hours, people with no dentist can also use the NHS Fife dental line: **[NHS FIFE DENTAL LINE]**. *The manager must check this number with NHS Fife before it is printed or read out.*
- Heavy bleeding, swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or a serious injury: tell them to call **999**.
- In Notes write only "signposted [date]". Tell the practice manager the same day.

## If nobody answers

You get **up to 3 attempts** for each person:

| Attempt | When |
|---|---|
| 1 | As soon as you can (same day, 8am to 8:45pm) |
| 2 | About 2 hours later, before 9pm |
| 3 | The next day, at a different time. Try once between 3pm and 9pm |

The sheet works out **Next call due** for you.

### Voicemail (after attempt 1 only)

> "Hi, it's [your name] from Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic. You joined our waiting list and I'm calling to take a few details. There's no need to call back. We'll try you again. You're still on the list. Thanks."

Don't leave health details or other family members' names on a voicemail.

### Missed-call text

After the first missed call, send the **missed-call text** (template M1 on page 2). Only between 8am and 9pm.

After 3 attempts with no answer, the sheet sets **Unreachable**. They stay on the list and still get service updates.

## After every call: pick a Call outcome

In **Call outcome** (column Y), pick one of these. The sheet then sets Status, counts the attempt and works out the next call. Then it clears the box, ready for next time.

| Call outcome | When to pick it | Status it sets |
|---|---|---|
| Reached - on list | You spoke and took their details | Called |
| Reached - call back | They asked you to ring back (tomorrow 10am) | Called |
| Reached - booked | They booked an appointment (after opening) | Booked |
| Reached - not interested | They don't want a place | Not interested |
| Already has dentist | They've found another dentist and want to leave the list. *(May be renamed "Found another dentist - leave list".)* | Not interested |
| No answer | It rang out | No answer |
| Voicemail | You left the voicemail | No answer |
| Busy/declined | It was busy or they rejected the call | No answer |
| Wrong number | The number isn't theirs or doesn't work | Wrong number |
| Withdrew | They want to come off the list for another reason (e.g. moved away) | Withdrew |
| DO NOT CONTACT | They asked us to stop contacting them | DO NOT CONTACT |

- Someone who **has** a dentist but still wants to join is "Reached - on list", not "Already has dentist". Having a dentist never counts against anyone.
- Don't type "Call 8am" or "Duplicate". The scripts set those.
- A row left blank or on "Call 8am" counts as **not called yet**, so always pick an outcome.

### Marketing consent

Leave **Marketing consent** as it is. Only change it to Yes if the person clearly asks for news and offers. Write the date and what they said in Notes. **Private interest**: fill in only if *they* raised private care. Never pitch private treatment.

## Opt-outs: DO NOT CONTACT

If anyone says "stop calling me", "take me off" or "don't text me":

1. Say: "No problem. I'll take you off now. Sorry to bother you."
2. Pick **DO NOT CONTACT** the **same day**. Use exactly those words.
3. In Notes, add the date and how they asked (phone, text, Facebook). No other details.
4. Never call, text or email them again. The scripts check for these words.

Do the same if you see a "stop" request by text, email, Facebook or Instagram.

## Tricky calls

**"When will I get in?"**

> "I can't give you a date yet. We invite people in stages as appointments become available. We'll keep you updated."

**"Is it really NHS?"**

> "Yes. We're a new NHS dental practice opening soon on Tolbooth Street. Joining our waiting list is how you'll be invited to register."

**"Why not me yet?"** Stay calm. Explain the stages. Offer to check their details. If someone is abusive, end the call politely and tell the practice manager.

**"Do you do implants or private treatment?"**

> "Yes, we offer private treatment too. It's completely separate and never affects your place on the NHS waiting list."

Don't say more unless they ask. Don't offer prices or deals.

**"Do I have to pay anything?"**

> "No. Joining is free, and the first NHS exam is free."

**"Is this a scam?"**

> "Good question to ask. We never ask for payment or bank details to join or for your first exam. You can check us at kirkcaldydental.co.uk."

**About photos:** the people in our ads and leaflets are stock or computer-made images. Never say they are our patients or staff.
