## 1. Purpose

Infection prevention and control is just as important on a domiciliary visit as in the practice, but there are extra difficulties: used instruments have to be transported, and hand hygiene facilities may be poor. This policy sets out how the practice manages those risks.

## 2. Scope

All clinicians and dental nurses who provide dental care in patients' homes, care homes or other settings away from 23–25 Tolbooth Street.

## 5. Policy and procedure

1. **Before the visit**: check the domiciliary kit using the checklist [attach]. Find out from the care home or carer about any known infections so the clinician can decide on Transmission Based Precautions.
2. **Transport** all equipment, instruments and PPE **securely** to and from the practice in a way that avoids contamination or damage. Clean and used items are kept in separate, clearly labelled, rigid, lidded containers.
3. **Hand hygiene**: take hand hygiene products for use during the visit. Products (wash or rub) must meet the obligatory and additional **BS EN standards** in the NIPCM Hand Hygiene Literature Review. Hand rub can be alcohol-based or non-alcohol based if it meets those standards. If a product does not meet them, complete a **risk assessment** to ensure safe systems of work. Take liquid soap and paper towels in case hands become visibly soiled.
4. **PPE**: take a full set of PPE (gloves, aprons, masks, eye protection, patient bib and eye protection).
5. **Single-use items**: where possible, use single-use items. Dispose of them in the appropriate waste containers on site (if the setting has a suitable healthcare waste stream) or **securely transport them back to the practice for disposal** in the correct waste stream (see 22-waste-management-policy.md).
6. **Sharps**: take a small, rigid, labelled sharps container with a temporary closure; close it for transport.
7. **Used reusable instruments**: where reusable items have been used **or unwrapped** during the visit, transport them back to the practice in a **designated secure container marked "USED MEDICAL EQUIPMENT"** for decontamination (SHTM 01-03). Process them through the LDU on return the same day.
8. **Surfaces**: lay out a clean, impervious, single-use barrier as a work surface. Clean and disinfect any reusable equipment surfaces (e.g. portable light, motor) before leaving.
9. **Spills**: carry a small spill kit; manage spills as in 27-cleaning-the-care-environment.md.
10. **Sharps injury or splash**: follow 29-occupational-exposure-and-sharps.md (first aid, report, OHS).
11. **Vehicle**: the transport containers are secured in the vehicle boot, away from the passenger area.

## 6. Records and monitoring

- Domiciliary kit checklist signed before and after each visit.
- Record in Dentally of each visit, including any infection risks noted.
- Log of used instruments returned to the LDU and processed.
- IPC Lead reviews domiciliary procedures [annually].

## 7. Training

Staff who carry out domiciliary visits are trained in this policy, safe transport of used instruments, and hand hygiene without a fixed basin, before their first visit.
