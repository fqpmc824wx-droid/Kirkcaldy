## Registering with us
NHS patients register with a named dentist. Registration with us ends any registration you have with another practice. Please bring a list of your medicines.

## Appointments, cancellations and missed appointments
Please give at least 48 hours' notice if you cannot attend. Repeated late cancellations or missed appointments may lead to deregistration (see our deregistration policy at reception and on our website).

## Emergencies and out of hours
- During opening hours: phone us on 01592 214225.
- Fife Dental Advice Line: 01592 226555 (Mon–Fri 8.30am–5pm).
- Out of hours: phone **NHS 24 on 111**.

## Fees
NHS charges are set by the Scottish Government. Our current NHS charges guide and private price list are displayed in the waiting room. You will get a written estimate before treatment starts.

## Interpreting
Free interpreting, including British Sign Language, is available for NHS care. Please ask when you book.

## Your information
We keep your records safely and confidentially. Our privacy notice is on our website and at reception.

## Comments, concerns and complaints
We welcome feedback. Please speak to any member of the team, or contact our Complaints Lead [Name] on 01592 214225 or info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk. If you are not satisfied with our response to an NHS complaint, you can contact the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman (0800 377 7330). For private treatment, you can contact the Dental Complaints Service (020 8253 0800).

## Zero tolerance
We will not tolerate violent or abusive behaviour towards our team or other patients.
