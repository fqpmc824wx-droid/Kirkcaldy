## 1. Purpose
NHS Fife and NHS Scotland send important information (safety alerts, PCA circulars, patient referrals, payment and list notices) to the practice **NHSmail** mailbox. This policy makes sure it is checked every working day and used securely.

## 2. Arrangements
1. The practice applies to NHS Fife for an NHSmail shared mailbox: [address once issued]@nhs.scot (or nhs.net).
2. **Named people** with access: [Name] (primary) and [Name] (deputy). Access is given to clinicians who need it.
3. The mailbox is checked **at least once every working day**, and before closing; the deputy checks when the primary is absent.
4. Urgent items (safety alerts, drug recalls, patient-safety notices, referral replies) are actioned the same day and shown to the dentist(s); other items are shared at the practice meeting.
5. NHSmail is used for **patient-identifiable information** sent to NHS bodies (referrals, NHS Fife). Ordinary practice email (info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk) is **not** used to send clinical information to other NHS services.
6. Passwords are never shared; two-factor authentication is on; leavers are removed the same day.
7. Emails relevant to a patient are saved into the patient's record in Dentally.

## 3. Inspection checklist
