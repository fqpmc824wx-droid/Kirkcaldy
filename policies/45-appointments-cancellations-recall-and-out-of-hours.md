## 1. Purpose
To explain clearly how patients book, cancel and are recalled, what happens when they miss appointments, how much NHS care costs, and where to get help when we are closed.

## 2. Booking
- Appointments are booked by phone (01592 214225), in person, or [online – if offered] and recorded in Dentally.
- Reminders are sent by [text/email] [24–48 hours] before the appointment.
- Opening hours: [to be decided]. Displayed at the entrance, on the website and in the patient leaflet.

## 3. Cancellations and missed appointments
- Please give at least **48 hours' notice** to cancel or change an appointment, so the time can be offered to another patient. (Same rule as the other Saint Visage Dental Group practices.)
- Late cancellations (less than 48 hours' notice) and missed appointments are recorded in Dentally.
- A patient who cancels with less than 48 hours' notice **three times** and fails to attend **twice** without notice may be deregistered, following the deregistration policy (47). The practice writes to the patient first to explain the rule.
- [Practice decision: any charge for missed private appointments, £[amount]. NHS rules on charging for missed appointments to be checked with NHS Fife before any charge is applied.]
- We consider each case fairly, including vulnerable patients and children (missed appointments for children may be a safeguarding indicator – see policy 12).

## 4. Recall
- Patients are recalled at intervals set by the dentist according to their risk (in line with SDCEP/NICE guidance), using Dentally reminders.
- The recall list is checked **[monthly]** by [Name].

## 5. NHS charges
- NHS patients pay **80% of the cost of treatment, up to a maximum of £384 per course of treatment** [check current figures with NHS Scotland before display]. Some patients are exempt (for example under-26s, pregnant women and new mothers, and people on certain benefits) [check current exemptions].
- The current NHS charges guide and our private price list are displayed in the waiting room and available on request. Patients get a written estimate before any course of treatment.
- Exemption evidence is checked at each course of treatment.

## 6. Urgent care and out of hours
- During opening hours, patients in pain should phone the practice; we aim to see registered patients with urgent problems the same day where possible.
- **Fife Dental Advice Line: 01592 226555** (Monday–Friday 8.30am–5pm) – for people with no dentist or who need advice.
- **Out of hours: NHS 24 on 111.**
- This information is on the answerphone, the website, the door and the patient leaflet.
