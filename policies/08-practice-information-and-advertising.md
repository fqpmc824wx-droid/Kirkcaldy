## 1. Purpose

Patients need accurate, up-to-date information about the practice, its team, its services and its charges. NHS practices in Scotland must provide a patient information leaflet containing the items set out in Schedule 5 of the NHS (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010. All advertising and publicity must follow the GDC Guidance on Advertising.

This policy sets out what Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic publishes, what it must contain, what must be displayed in the practice, and the rules for all advertising, including the website kirkcaldydental.co.uk and social media.

## 2. Scope

- The patient information leaflet (printed and on the website).
- Notices displayed in the practice.
- The website, social media, online listings, newsletters, signage and any paid advertising.
- Promotional material for private services, including implants.
- All staff who write, approve or post practice information, and every registrant named in it.

## 5. Policy / procedure

### 5.1 Patient information leaflet – mandatory (Schedule 5) items

The practice produces a patient information leaflet containing **at least** the following Schedule 5 items (marked "a" in the PSM). Use the PSM Patient Information Leaflet Items and Example templates (Aug 2025).

**Dentists' personal and professional details** (including partners, associates and assistants):

- name, including first name, as it appears on the dental register;
- date of registration and GDC-recognised dental qualifications;
- whether the dentist provides only orthodontic treatment;
- whether a dental hygienist or dental therapist is employed at the practice.

**Contact information, hours and access:**

- address and telephone numbers of the practice (and the registered name and address if the practice is a corporate body): Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW; [practice phone – not yet set]; info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk; [registered company name and address if applicable];
- address for correspondence;
- opening hours, including the days and hours each dentist is usually at the practice;
- arrangements for care if the patient's registered dentist is not available;
- whether there is access to the surgery without stairs;
- accessibility of the premises, including surgery and toilets, to wheelchair users;
- languages other than English spoken by each dentist (in line with each dentist's wishes) and how language needs can be met;
- policy on cancelling appointments and charges for non-attendance;
- out-of-hours care arrangements;
- arrangements for emergency appointments;
- consequences of termination of a capitation arrangement (see `05-nhs-registration-deregistration-change-of-dentist.md`).

**Treatment and charges:**

- care and treatment available under general dental services;
- explanation of NHS charges and exemptions;
- repairs and replacements provided free of charge.

**Additional information:**

- an explanation of the importance of regular dental care.

### 5.2 Patient information – National Standards items

The National Standards for Dental Services (marked "b" in the PSM) list further items that should be easily accessible. The practice includes them in the leaflet or provides them on the website, posters or supplementary leaflets:

- directions to the practice;
- how the practice meets additional needs (e.g. hearing induction loop, interpreter, large-print literature on request);
- practice policy on accepting new patients;
- how to book an appointment and any reminder system (Dentally text/email reminders);
- whether NHS only or NHS and private care are offered, and any insurance or payment plan schemes;
- payment options;
- charges for failing to attend;
- a statement on how to give feedback or make a complaint (see `01-complaints-feedback-procedure.md`);
- a statement that the practice's inspection report and details of quality improvement measures, such as audit, are available on request.

The PSM also lists a statement that all personal information is kept confidential in line with the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR. Include it.

### 5.5 Advertising rules

All advertising and publicity, including the leaflet, website and social media, must be **legal, decent, honest and truthful** (GDC Guidance on Advertising). False or misleading material, or material with the potential to mislead, may lead to a GDC fitness to practise investigation and can be a criminal offence.

The GDC guidance covers advertising services, endorsing products, websites (including marketing websites), use of specialist titles, and use of honorary degrees and memberships. The practice applies these rules:

1. **Accuracy.** Every claim must be accurate and capable of being supported. No guarantees of outcomes. [Check current GDC guidance on testimonials, before-and-after images and comparisons.]
2. **Specialist titles.** Only describe a dentist as a "specialist" if they are on the relevant GDC specialist list. Do not use wording that implies specialist status otherwise (for example, describe implant experience factually rather than as "implant specialist" unless on a specialist list). Check current GDC guidance.
3. **Qualifications and memberships.** Only list GDC-recognised qualifications accurately. Do not present honorary degrees or memberships in a way that could mislead. Check current GDC guidance.
4. **Registrant details.** Where a registrant's name appears (including dental care professionals), the registrant checks the information is correct.
5. **Website.** Follow the GDC guidance for websites. [Practice decision/check current GDC guidance: website to show practice name and address, GDC details for each registrant, the complaints procedure, and the date the site was last updated.]
6. **Prices and offers.** Advertised prices must be clear and complete, including what is and is not included. Offers must not pressure patients into treatment.
7. **Products.** Endorse products only in line with the GDC guidance and disclose any financial interest.
8. **Botulinum toxin.** Botulinum toxin injections are a prescription-only medicine and **cannot be advertised to the public**, including on social media, even if given by a registered healthcare professional. See the Advertising Standards Authority guidance.
9. **NHS.** Do not use the NHS logo. Plain text "NHS" may be used to describe NHS services.
10. **Patient images and reviews.** Use patient images or comments only with written consent, in line with the data protection policy.

### 5.6 Approval process

1. The author drafts the material.
2. Each named registrant checks their details.
3. The Practice Manager checks it against this policy.
4. The Practice Owner approves it.
5. Record the item, date and approver in the advertising log.
