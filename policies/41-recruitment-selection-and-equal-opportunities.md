## 1. Purpose
To recruit the right people fairly and safely, and to treat every applicant and team member equally. Because staff work with children and adults at risk, safe recruitment protects patients.

## 2. Scope
All posts: dentists (employed or self-employed associates), dental nurses, hygienists/therapists, reception, practice manager, cleaners and locums.

## 3. Legislation and guidance
- Equality Act 2010 (protected characteristics: age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, sexual orientation)
- Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act 2006 (right-to-work checks)
- Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 and Disclosure (Scotland) Act 2020 (PVG scheme)
- GDC Standards for the Dental Team; UK GDPR (candidate data)
- See also 13-pvg-scheme-and-safe-recruitment.md

## 5. Procedure
1. **Job description and person specification** written for each post (essential and desirable criteria only; nothing that discriminates).
2. **Advert** states the job, hours, pay range, closing date and that we are an equal opportunities employer. No wording that excludes protected groups.
3. **Shortlisting** against the person specification, by two people where possible. Reasons recorded.
4. **Interview** with the same core questions for every candidate. Reasonable adjustments offered (for example step-free access or extra time). Scores and notes kept.
5. **Conditional offer**, subject to all checks below. No start date until every check is complete.
6. **Pre-employment checks** (record dates in the staff file):
   - right to work in the UK (original documents seen and copied);
   - **two references**, one from the most recent employer;
   - **GDC registration** checked on the GDC online register (registrants) and indemnity confirmed;
   - **PVG scheme membership** (or a new application) through Disclosure Scotland – 0300 020 0040; nobody works unsupervised with patients until the check is back;
   - **health clearance and hepatitis B status** (see 30-health-clearance-and-immunisation.md);
   - qualifications and CPD certificates seen;
   - for NHS dentists: on (or applying to) the NHS Fife dental list.
7. **Written contract or statement of particulars** issued on or before day one (see policy 42).
8. **Induction** using the PSM New Staff Induction Checklist; staff read and sign all policies (see policy 42).

## 6. Equal opportunities
- We do not discriminate in recruitment, pay, training, promotion or dismissal.
- We make reasonable adjustments for disabled applicants and staff.
- Bullying, harassment and victimisation are not tolerated; concerns go to the Practice Owner or through the raising concerns policy (07).
- Recruitment data is kept securely and deleted for unsuccessful candidates after **6 months**.

## 7. Records
Staff file per person: application, interview notes, offer, ID and right-to-work copy, references, GDC and indemnity check, PVG, health clearance, contract, induction checklist, training. Kept locked or in a restricted folder; retained for 6 years after employment ends.

## 8. Inspection checklist

- [ ] Staff files show references, ID/right to work, GDC check, indemnity, PVG and health clearance for every team member
- [ ] No one worked with patients before checks were complete
