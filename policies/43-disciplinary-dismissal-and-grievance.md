## 1. Purpose
To deal with conduct, performance and staff complaints fairly and consistently, following the ACAS Code of Practice on Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures.

## 2. Principles
- Issues are dealt with promptly and informally where possible.
- Facts are investigated before any decision.
- The employee is told the problem in writing and has the chance to respond at a meeting.
- The employee may bring a **companion** (a colleague or trade union representative).
- Decisions are confirmed in writing, with the **right of appeal**.
- Concerns about patient safety go through 07-raising-concerns-whistleblowing.md; GDC fitness-to-practise concerns may need reporting to the GDC.

## 3. Disciplinary procedure
1. **Informal stage:** a private conversation and a note on file for minor issues.
2. **Investigation** by someone not involved in the issue.
3. **Disciplinary meeting:** written invitation with the evidence, at least 48 hours' notice.
4. **Outcomes:** no action; first written warning (on file 6 months); final written warning (12 months); dismissal (with notice) or, for gross misconduct, summary dismissal.
5. **Gross misconduct** includes: theft or fraud, violence, serious breach of confidentiality or data protection, serious breach of infection control or patient safety, working while unfit through drink or drugs, harassment, falsifying records, working without GDC registration or PVG clearance.
6. Suspension on full pay may be used during an investigation; it is not a disciplinary action.
7. **Appeal** in writing within 5 working days, heard by someone more senior where possible.

## 4. Performance (capability)
Support first: clear targets, training and a review period, then the same meeting and warning steps if there is no improvement. Health-related capability involves occupational health.

## 5. Grievance procedure
1. Raise informally with the Practice Manager, or in writing if not resolved (to the Practice Owner if the grievance is about the Practice Manager).
2. Grievance meeting within 5 working days; companion allowed.
3. Written outcome; appeal within 5 working days.

## 6. Records
All notes, letters and outcomes kept confidentially in the staff file; warnings disregarded after the stated period.
