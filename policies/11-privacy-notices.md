## 1. Purpose

Data protection law requires us to tell people, before we start using their information, what we hold about them, why, our lawful basis, who we share it with, how long we keep it and their rights, including the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). This document holds the three privacy notices the practice uses, ready to adapt:

- **Notice A** – Patients (and people on our waiting list)
- **Notice B** – Children (age-appropriate version)
- **Notice C** – Staff, associates and job applicants

## 2. Scope

These notices cover all personal information processed by Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, including in Dentally, Google Workspace, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) lead forms, ClickSend SMS, imaging software and paper records. They must match the information audit and lawful bases in 10-data-protection-confidentiality-information-security.md.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Before publishing

1. Fill in every `[bracket]` and delete what does not apply.
2. Check each notice against the ICO privacy notice checklist. The PSM says every notice must include: practice owner name and contact details; DPO contact details; the data held; why it is held; the lawful basis; what we do with it; who we share it with; how long we keep it; how it is destroyed; and the right to complain to the ICO.
3. Have the DPO review them.
4. Produce a large-print (16 point) version and keep a copy at reception (see 09-disability-equality-reasonable-adjustments.md).

### 5.2 Making the notices available

- **Patients**: link on every page footer of kirkcaldydental.co.uk ("Privacy"), linked from Meta lead forms and online booking/forms; displayed in the waiting room; printed copy given to new patients at registration.
- **Children**: given with the patient notice to child patients and their parents; displayed in the waiting room [at child height]; consider adding pictures or cartoons.
- **Staff**: given at induction and discussed at a practice meeting; given to job applicants with the application information.

### 5.3 Review

Review at least yearly and whenever processing changes (for example if CCTV is installed, a new SMS provider is used, or the PSM/ICO guidance changes after the DUAA 2025 review). Record the review date on each notice.

### Notice A – Patient privacy notice

**Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic – How we use your personal information**

*Last updated: [date]*

**Who we are**
Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW, provides NHS dental care (under NHS Fife) and private dental care, including dental implants. The data controller is Lang Toun Smiles Ltd, owned by Nas [surname], Principal Dentist. Contact: info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk, 01592 214225. ICO registration number: [number].

**Our Data Protection Officer**
You can contact our Data Protection Officer about anything in this notice: [dpo email], [address/phone].

**What information we hold about you**

- Your name, address, date of birth, phone numbers, email address, and your NHS Community Health Index (CHI) number.
- Your doctor's details and next of kin or emergency contact.
- Your medical history, medicines and allergies.
- Your dental records: examination notes, treatment plans and treatment given, X-rays, photographs, 3D scans and implant planning records, and letters to and from other health professionals.
- Your appointments, attendance and communications with us (including calls, emails and texts).
- Payment information and NHS charge and exemption details. [We do not store full card numbers.]
- Your consent forms and any preferences you have told us, such as communication or access needs.
- [CCTV images, if CCTV is installed – see our CCTV notice.]

**Where we get your information**
Mostly from you. We may also receive information from your doctor, a previous dentist, hospitals or specialists you have been referred to, NHS Fife and Practitioner Services Division (PSD), or a parent, carer or legal representative.

**Why we hold it and our lawful basis**

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Providing your NHS dental care | Public task. Because this includes health information, we also rely on the special category condition for providing health care. |
| Providing your private dental care, including implants | Contract. Special category condition: providing health care. |
| Making and reminding you about appointments and recalls (by text, email, phone or letter) | Legitimate interests – it is necessary for your care and you would expect it. [Confirm with DPO.] |
| NHS claims, payments, accounts and tax | Legal obligation |
| Meeting NHS, GDC, health and safety and other legal requirements, including inspections | Legal obligation / public task |
| Sending you news and offers (only if you opt in) | Consent – you can withdraw at any time |

**Waiting list (before we open)**
If you registered interest through our website, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, or by phone, we hold your name, phone number, email and the treatment you are interested in. We use it only to tell you when we open and how to register, and, if you separately opted in, to send you offers by text or email. Our lawful basis is your consent. Lead forms are run on Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and our text messages are sent through ClickSend. You can reply STOP to any text or email [info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk] to be removed. We delete waiting-list details when you register as a patient (your information then becomes part of your patient record), when you ask us to, or by [date / 6 months after opening].

**Who we share your information with**
We share only the minimum information needed, and only with people who need to know:

- other health professionals involved in your care: your doctor, hospitals, specialists, the Public Dental Service, and dental laboratories making your crowns, dentures, aligners or implant parts
- NHS Fife, Practitioner Services Division (PSD) and Dental Reference Officers, as required for NHS dental services
- our suppliers, who process data for us under contract: Dentally (patient records software), Google Workspace (email and documents), ClickSend (text messages), [IT support company], [payment provider], [finance provider for private treatment, only if you apply], [confidential waste company]
- [implant system or planning software suppliers – name]
- regulators and authorities where the law requires it, for example the GDC, or police with appropriate legal authority
- other organisations where there is a risk of serious harm to you or others, for example child or adult protection services.

If we need to share your information for any other reason, we will ask for your consent first.

**Information stored outside the UK**
Some of our suppliers may store data outside the UK [state which – for example Meta, ClickSend, Google]. Where this happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by UK law.

**How long we keep it**

- Dental records: 10 years from your last contact with us (for adults and children). If a patient dies, we keep records for 3 years; if a child dies before their 17th birthday, until they would have turned 25.
- Financial records: [6 years].
- Waiting-list details: see above.
- Marketing preferences: until you withdraw consent.

**How we keep it safe and destroy it**
Records are held securely in Dentally (cloud) and on password-protected, encrypted systems with individual logins. Paper is kept in locked cabinets. When information is no longer needed, paper is shredded or incinerated by a confidential waste company, and electronic data is securely deleted or the device destroyed.

**Your rights**
You have the right to:

- be told how we use your information (this notice)
- get a copy of your information (a subject access request) – free of charge, normally within one month. You can ask in writing or verbally.
- ask us to correct information that is wrong
- ask us to delete information or restrict its use – but we must keep dental records for the period above for legal and professional reasons
- ask us to send your electronic records to another dentist in a commonly used format
- object to how we use your information, including marketing
- not be subject to decisions made only by automated means (we do not do this).

If you are aged 12 or over, you can usually make a request about your own records yourself.

**Complaints**
Please contact us or our Data Protection Officer first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113, Information Commissioner's Office – Scotland, [address].

### Notice B – Privacy notice for children

**Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic – Your information and you**

*Last updated: [date]. A grown-up can help you read this.*

**Who are we?**
We are Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, the dentist at 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy. Nas is in charge. If you have a question, ask us at reception or email info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk. You can also contact our Data Protection Officer, who makes sure we look after your information properly: [dpo email].

**What do we know about you?**

- your name, birthday, address and the people who look after you
- your health – for example if you have asthma or take medicine
- what we see when we check your teeth, X-rays (pictures of the inside of your teeth), photos, and the treatment we give you
- when you come to see us.

**Why do we need it?**
So we can look after your teeth safely and properly, and remind you and your family about your next visit. The law lets us use your information to look after your health. We need to keep it to do our job.

**Who do we share it with?**
Only people who need it to help look after you, like your doctor, a hospital or specialist dentist if you need to see one, the dental laboratory that makes things like braces, and the NHS. We also use computer companies that keep our records safe, like Dentally. If we ever think you might not be safe, we may need to tell people whose job is to keep children safe. We will try to tell you first.

**How long do we keep it?**
We keep your dental records for 10 years after your last visit. Your records will grow up with you and become your adult records.

**How do we keep it safe?**
Your information is kept on secure computers with passwords. Only the people who look after you can see it.

**Your rights**
It is your information. You can:

- ask to see what we have written about you. If you are 12 or older you can usually ask yourself. If you are younger, a parent or carer can usually ask for you.
- ask us to fix something that is wrong
- ask us questions about how we use your information
- tell us if you are not happy.

If your parent or carer said yes to something for you (for example, getting our newsletter), you can change your mind when you are old enough to decide. We do not send adverts to children.

**Not happy?**
Tell us, or ask a grown-up to help you. You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office, who checks that organisations look after information properly: ico.org.uk.

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Recruitment, employment, pay, contracts | Contract; steps before entering a contract |
| Tax, National Insurance, pensions, right to work, PVG checks, health and safety, NHS and GDC requirements | Legal obligation |
| Health and immunisation records, sickness absence, adjustments | Legal obligation / contract. Special category condition: employment, and occupational health [confirm with DPO]. |
| PVG and criminal record information | Legal obligation under the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 and Disclosure (Scotland) Act 2020 |

**Who we share it with**

- payroll provider [name], accountant [name], pension provider [name], HMRC
- NHS Fife and Practitioner Services Division (for dentists on the NHS dental list and NHS claims)
- the GDC, Disclosure Scotland, occupational health provider [name], indemnity providers, where required
- IT and software suppliers who process data for us (Dentally, Google Workspace)
- [HR adviser – name]

**How long we keep it**

- Unsuccessful applicants: [6 months] after the recruitment decision.
- Staff records: [6 years after employment ends – confirm with HR adviser/DPO].
