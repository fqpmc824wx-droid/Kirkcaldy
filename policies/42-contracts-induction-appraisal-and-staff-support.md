## 1. Purpose
To make sure every team member has a written contract, a proper induction, regular appraisal and support, and that practice meetings are held and minuted.

## 2. Legislation and guidance
- Employment Rights Act 1996 (written statement of particulars on or before day one)
- Working Time Regulations 1998; National Minimum Wage Act 1998
- Pensions Act 2008 (auto-enrolment); HMRC PAYE
- GDC Standards (supervision, CPD); NES/PSM guidance on induction and appraisal

## 3. Contracts
1. Employees get a **written statement of employment particulars on or before their first day** (job title, start date, pay, hours, holidays, sick pay, notice, place of work, pension, disciplinary and grievance procedures).
2. Self-employed associates and locums have a **written associate/locum agreement** (BDA template or equivalent), including indemnity and NHS list obligations.
3. Signed copies are kept in the staff file; the employee keeps a copy.
4. Payroll (HMRC PAYE) and **workplace pension auto-enrolment** are set up before the first pay day.

## 4. Induction
1. Every new starter completes the **PSM New Staff Induction Training Checklist** in their first week, covering: fire and evacuation, medical emergencies, infection control and decontamination, sharps injuries, waste, COSHH, data protection and confidentiality, safeguarding, complaints, raising concerns, lone working, the practice software.
2. New starters **read and sign every policy** (the staff sign-off tables). Clinical staff complete role-specific decontamination and radiography induction.
3. A named buddy supports them; the Practice Manager signs off the checklist.

## 5. Appraisal and personal development
1. Each team member has an **annual appraisal** (and a review at the end of any probation period), recorded on a form and signed by both people.
2. The appraisal reviews performance, training needs and the **personal development plan**; GDC registrants' CPD is checked against GDC requirements.
3. Next appraisal round: [date].

## 6. Staff support and wellbeing
- Open-door access to the Practice Owner and Practice Manager.
- Support after difficult incidents (violence, sharps injury, complaint, significant event): time out, debrief, and referral to occupational health (NHS Fife Occupational Health & Wellbeing, 01592 729401) where needed.
- Reasonable adjustments and flexible working requests considered fairly.

## 7. Practice meetings
- Whole-team meetings at least **[monthly]**; minutes taken by [Name], stored in [location], and read by absent staff.
- Standing items: incidents and significant events, complaints and feedback, infection control, health and safety, policy updates, training.
