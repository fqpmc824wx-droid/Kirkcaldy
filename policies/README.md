# Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic – Practice Policies

23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW · NHS Fife · info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk

These policies follow the **Scottish Dental Practice Support Manual (PSM)**, published by the Scottish Dental
Clinical Effectiveness Programme (SDCEP) and NHS Education for Scotland: https://www.psm.sdcep.org.uk.
The PSM is the reference that NHS Fife uses for the **Combined Practice Inspection (CPI)**, which every NHS
practice must pass before and after opening (then every 3 years). Each policy names the PSM section it is based on,
lists the PSM templates to download and adapt, and ends with an inspection checklist.

> **Status: draft for adoption (version 1.0).** The practice is not open yet. Before adopting:
> 1. Fill in every `[square bracket]` (names, dates, phone numbers, contractors, equipment).
> 2. Delete procedures that don't apply (e.g. CCTV, domiciliary visits, CBCT) and add any that do.
> 3. The Practice Owner signs and dates each policy; every team member reads it and signs the sign-off table.
> 4. Check against the latest **CPI checklist** on the Scottish Dental website (scottishdental.nhs.scot) –
>    the PSM changes several times a year (see its News page).
>
> These documents are practice templates, not legal advice. Have your indemnity provider, RPA and NHS Fife
> dental practice adviser confirm anything you are unsure about.

## Before-opening checklist (from the PSM)

- [ ] All 40 policies adapted, signed and dated; staff sign-off sheets complete
- [ ] NHS Fife: practice and every dentist on the dental list; practice inspection booked
- [ ] ICO data protection fee paid; privacy notices on the website and in reception
- [ ] HSE notified of radiation work (IRR17); RPA appointed in writing; critical examination of X-ray units done
- [ ] Fire safety risk assessment; legionella risk assessment; health & safety and COSHH risk assessments
- [ ] Pressure vessel written scheme of examination and insurance inspection (compressor, autoclaves)
- [ ] Clinical waste contract, pre-acceptance audit and consignment notes in place
- [ ] Emergency drugs, oxygen, AED and kit in place with weekly log; team medical emergency training
- [ ] Decontamination equipment validated; daily/weekly test records started
- [ ] Every team member: GDC registration, indemnity, PVG membership, hepatitis B / health clearance, CPD record
- [ ] Patient information leaflet, complaints poster, zero tolerance poster and pregnancy poster displayed
- [ ] Access survey done; hearing loop / reasonable adjustments in place
- [ ] Business continuity plan with key contacts printed and kept off-site
