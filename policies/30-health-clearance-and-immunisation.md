## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how the practice makes sure that:

- staff are protected against infections they may be exposed to at work, including hepatitis B (immunisation);
- patients are protected from infections that could be passed on by healthcare workers, including bloodborne viruses (BBVs) and tuberculosis (TB) (health clearance);
- staff health information is kept confidential.

Safe working practices are the most effective way of reducing risk. **Immunisation is never a substitute for good practice** such as hand hygiene, PPE and safe sharps handling.

## 2. Scope

All staff: dentists (including implant surgeons, associates, locums and visiting clinicians), dental hygienists and therapists, dental nurses (qualified and trainee), staff who decontaminate instruments, reception and administrative staff, and cleaners. It applies before a person starts work and throughout their employment or engagement.

## 5. Policy and procedure

### 5.1 Occupational Health arrangements

1. NHS Boards provide occupational health services to dentists with an NHS list number and to staff in practices providing NHS treatment (CDO letter, 2018). Provision may differ between Boards.
2. The Practice Manager confirms with **NHS Fife OHS** what is available to our staff, eligibility, how to refer, and costs for any private-only staff [NHS Fife Occupational Health & Wellbeing: 01592 729401, fife.occhealth@nhs.scot].
3. For any staff not covered by NHS Fife OHS, the practice uses [private OHS provider / the staff member's GP].

### 5.2 Assessing each post for EPPs

1. **Exposure prone procedures (EPPs)** are procedures where injury to the healthcare worker could result in their blood contaminating the patient's open tissues ("bleed-back"): where gloved hands may be in contact with sharp instruments, needle tips or sharp tissues inside a patient's mouth where the fingertips may not be completely visible.
2. UKAP categorises dental procedures into EPP levels 0–3. For example, local anaesthetic injection is **Level 1** and routine tooth extraction is **Level 2**; there are no Level 3 general dentistry procedures. Implant placement and other oral surgery are EPPs.
3. The Practice Owner and IPC Lead **risk-assess each post** and record whether it involves EPPs:

| Post | Involves EPPs? | Clearance needed |
|---|---|---|
| Dentist / implant surgeon | Yes | Standard + Additional |
| Dental hygienist / therapist | Yes | Standard + Additional |
| Dental nurse | Not routinely (GDC scope does not include EPPs) – but **yes** if extended duties involve EPPs (e.g. suture removal where fingers are not visible) | Standard; Additional if EPP duties |
| Decontamination / LDU staff | No | Standard |
| Reception / admin / cleaner | No (no direct clinical contact) | Routine immunisation based on risk; HBV if they may contact blood (e.g. handling sharps bins or specimens) |

4. If unsure whether a procedure is an EPP, contact the **UKAP Secretariat**.

### 5.3 Health screening before starting work

1. All clinical healthcare workers (NHS and non-NHS), including dental nurses, complete the **pre-employment occupational health screening questionnaire** provided by NHS Fife before starting. It shows whether further checks are needed.
2. **Standard Health Clearance** applies to all new, first-time or returning healthcare workers (including students) with clinical contact, and is completed **before clinical duties begin**:
   - checks for TB disease or TB immunity;
   - offer of screening/immunisation for hepatitis B;
   - offer of testing for hepatitis C;
   - offer of testing for HIV.
3. **Additional Health Clearance** applies to all **new healthcare workers who will perform EPPs**, in addition to standard clearance. It includes testing for hepatitis B, hepatitis C and HIV and **must be completed before confirmation of employment** in an EPP post.
4. A "new healthcare worker" includes: someone with direct clinical contact with patients for the first time (NHS or independent sector, employee or by agreement, e.g. student placement); an existing worker moving into EPP work for the first time; and someone returning to NHS work who may have been exposed to a BBV while away (OHS risk assessment).
5. A worker **moving between EPP posts** provides evidence of their health clearance. If they cannot, they are treated as a new healthcare worker. Those who were practising EPPs **before 1 August 2008** do not have to provide evidence of clearance when moving from one EPP post to another.
6. New or existing workers applying for EPP work for the first time who **decline** BBV testing **will not be cleared to perform EPPs**.
7. Healthcare workers with BBVs who move to the UK cannot be tested immediately (HIV: wait 3 months; HBV: wait 4 weeks) and must not perform EPPs during this time.

### 5.4 Immunisation

Following the Green Book, as summarised in the PSM:

1. **Hepatitis B**: offered to all staff involved in clinical procedures or who may have direct contact with patients' blood or blood-stained body fluids, including those at risk of injury from blood-contaminated sharps and **staff involved in instrument decontamination**. HBV immunisation is recommended for all staff in these roles.
2. **Routine immunisations**: all staff with regular clinical contact and directly involved in patient care should be up to date with tetanus, diphtheria, polio, and measles, mumps and rubella (MMR). **Varicella, seasonal influenza and BCG (TB)** should also be considered.
3. **Non-clinical staff**: routine immunisations as above, based on assessment of risk.
4. The Practice Manager obtains the latest advice from NHS Fife OHS, including the **level of anti-HBs antibody required** (this can vary by region) and **advice on non-responders**.
5. A staff member who declines vaccination signs a declaration [form]. The IPC Lead and OHS review whether any duties need to change.
6. Boosters and follow-up checks are arranged as OHS advises. The Practice Manager keeps a reminder diary (dates only, not health details).

### 5.5 Confidentiality of staff health information

1. Healthcare workers have the same right to confidentiality as any patient. OHS records are held separately from employment records.
2. The practice **does not keep** details of vaccination history, immunity or BBV status. It keeps only the **OHS notice of fitness for clinical work** ("fit slip") and, where relevant, fitness to perform EPPs.
3. If the practice needs evidence of an employee's immune status, it first **obtains the employee's consent** to approach their GP or OHS, and stores the information securely and confidentially in line with the Data Protection Act 2018.
4. Fitness notices are kept in a locked cabinet / restricted electronic folder [location], accessible only to the Practice Owner and Practice Manager.
5. If patients are, or have been, at risk, it may be necessary in the public interest for the employer to have access to confidential information; this is handled with OHS advice.

### 5.6 Staff who may have a BBV or other serious infection

1. All staff must **seek occupational health or medical advice and testing, and/or notify the Practice Owner, without delay** if they know or suspect they have a BBV or another serious infection such as TB.
2. A healthcare worker who performs EPPs and has any reason to think they may have been exposed to a BBV (at work or outside work) must seek advice and/or a test through their GP, a sexual health clinic or OHS.
3. The Practice Owner seeks advice from **NHS Fife OHS** to make sure the staff member can access occupational health care, and to decide whether their working practice needs to be restricted.
4. Healthcare workers living with HBV, HCV or HIV may perform EPPs depending on their infective status and monitoring, as set out in the UKHSA integrated guidance. Decisions for HBV are made case by case. Viral load must continue to be monitored and reviewed by an accredited specialist in occupational medicine/OHS. (Registration with UKAP-OHR is no longer required since April 2024.)
5. The practice **keeps a record of procedures performed** by staff living with HIV or HBV who are cleared to perform EPPs. Self-employed staff with a BBV maintain their own record.
6. Any restriction is handled confidentially and supportively.

### 5.7 Locums, associates and students

Before their first session, the Practice Manager sees evidence of the locum's or associate's health clearance (OHS fitness notice, and EPP clearance where relevant) and hepatitis B immunity confirmation from OHS, without keeping detailed health data.
