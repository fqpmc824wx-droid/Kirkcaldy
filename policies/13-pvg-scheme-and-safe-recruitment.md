## 1. Purpose

This policy makes sure that nobody barred from working with children or protected adults works in a regulated role at Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, and that the practice meets its legal duties under the Protecting Vulnerable Groups (PVG) scheme. It also sets out the safe recruitment checks for every new team member.

## 2. Scope

All people who work at the practice: employees, self-employed associates, hygienists and therapists, locums (agency and independent), trainees, and non-clinical staff. It applies at recruitment, during employment, and when someone leaves.

## 3. Legislation and guidance

- Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (the PVG Act) – established the PVG scheme, administered by Disclosure Scotland
- Disclosure (Scotland) Act 2020 – from April 2025 PVG membership became a legal requirement for those doing a regulated role; "regulated work" was replaced by "regulated roles"
- From April 2026, lifetime PVG membership ended and was replaced by a **5-year membership that must be renewed every 5 years**
- Disclosure Scotland: About the PVG scheme; Roles that need PVG membership; Types of PVG application; Barred lists; What is a referral; Keep your details up to date; Code of Practice
- GDC – CPD in safeguarding children and young people, and safeguarding vulnerable adults
- Equality Act 2010 (no health questions before a job offer – see 09-disability-equality-reasonable-adjustments.md)

## 5. Policy and procedure

### 5.1 Key definitions

- **Vulnerable groups**: children (under 18) and protected adults. **All adults are protected adults while receiving dental treatment.**
- **Regulated role**: all dental professionals who provide clinical care are in a regulated role and must be PVG scheme members. This includes dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists, dental nurses (including trainees) and clinical dental technicians [and any other clinical role – check Disclosure Scotland "Roles that need PVG membership"].
- **Listed (barred)**: a person on the PVG Children's List and/or Adults' List. They are not eligible for PVG membership and must not do a regulated role.
- **Continuous monitoring**: Disclosure Scotland monitors members and tells all interested parties (for example employers) if a member becomes listed.

### 5.2 Who needs what check

| Role | Check | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Dentists, hygienists, therapists, dental nurses, trainee dental nurses, other clinical staff | PVG scheme membership for children and protected adults | **Mandatory** |
| Receptionists, practice manager (not in regulated roles; have access to patients) | Not eligible for PVG. Level 2 Disclosure at the employer's discretion | Optional – [Practice decision: we request a Level 2 Disclosure for all staff with patient access] |
| Staff with no access to patients (for example out-of-hours cleaner) | Not eligible for Level 2. Level 1 Disclosure may be requested | Optional – [Practice decision] |
| Agency locums | Assurance from the **agency** that the locum is a PVG member for the relevant groups and not barred | Mandatory to obtain assurance |
| Independent (self-employed) locums | Check the locum's eligibility to perform a regulated role **before** they start; it is the worker's responsibility to hold current PVG membership | Mandatory |
| Self-employed associates, hygienists, therapists under contract | The practice (organisation) starts the PVG application for them | Mandatory |

### 5.3 Types of PVG application

1. **PVG (Join)** – staff new to regulated roles joining the scheme for the first time, or existing members who will work with a new vulnerable group.
2. **PVG (Existing)** – existing members already in regulated roles and continuing with the same groups.
3. **Confirmation of PVG scheme membership (Join)** – self-employed staff in regulated roles joining for the first time, or adding a new vulnerable group.
4. **Confirmation of PVG scheme membership (Existing)** – self-employed staff continuing with the same groups.

Dental staff need membership covering **both** children and protected adults.

### 5.4 Applying for PVG membership

1. **Start the application**: the practice either registers as an accredited body with Disclosure Scotland, or enrols with an umbrella body that makes applications for us. [Practice decision: accredited body / umbrella body – name, contact, account number.] Note: NHS Fife may also offer this route for practices – [check].
2. The accredited body initiates the application. The individual receives an email link and has **14 days** to complete it. They need to sign in to or set up a **ScotAccount**.
3. If someone is self-employed and doing a regulated role under contract for the practice, the practice starts the application. Personal assistants and those working for an individual use a different process (see Disclosure Scotland).
4. When processed, the individual receives a secure link to view their disclosure. There is **no paper certificate**. They share the link with the accredited body and the practice.
5. If convictions are listed, the individual has **10 days** to ask Disclosure Scotland to remove certain convictions, before sharing the record with the employer.
6. The Practice Manager views the record and confirms the person is not listed. **The only way to confirm someone is not barred is to check their PVG scheme membership disclosure record held by Disclosure Scotland.**

### 5.5 Safe recruitment steps

1. Advertise the role with a clear job description and person specification, and say that PVG membership (or a Disclosure check) is required.
2. Use an application form; ask about adjustments needed for interview. No health questions before an offer (Equality Act 2010).
3. Interview with at least [two] people. Explore gaps in employment history.
4. Make any offer **conditional** on:
   - PVG membership (or Disclosure) satisfactory
   - two satisfactory references, including the most recent employer [and for clinical staff, a clinical reference]
   - proof of identity and right to work in the UK
   - GDC registration checked on the online register (registrants)
   - professional indemnity confirmed (registrants – see 16-ethical-practice-gdc-standards.md)
   - NHS Fife dental list inclusion (dentists providing NHS care)
   - occupational health clearance and immunisation (see 30-health-clearance-and-immunisation.md)
   - qualifications and CPD records checked.
5. **Nobody starts in a regulated role until the PVG check is confirmed.** It is an offence for an employer to offer or knowingly recruit a listed person to a regulated role, and an offence for a listed person to accept or carry out one.
6. Induction includes this policy, 12-child-and-adult-protection.md, confidentiality and raising concerns.

### 5.6 During employment

1. The Practice Manager keeps the **PVG register**: name, role, membership number, groups covered, date checked, membership expiry/renewal date (5-year cycle), and accredited body.
2. Renewal: the Practice Manager diarises renewal [6 months] before each member's 5-year expiry and supports the renewal application.
3. PVG and Disclosure records contain personal information. They are shared only with people entitled to see them, in line with the Disclosure Scotland Code of Practice. Store only what is needed (for example membership number and date checked), securely and with restricted access.
4. Locum assurance is checked before each new placement.

### 5.7 Responsibilities of PVG scheme members

Members must:

- tell Disclosure Scotland of any change of **name or gender within three months** (one month if they are under consideration for listing or are listed)
- tell Disclosure Scotland of a change of employer, address, email or phone number as soon as possible
- renew membership every 5 years
- tell the Practice Manager of anything that may affect their PVG status.

### 5.8 When someone leaves

The Practice Manager **informs Disclosure Scotland** when a PVG scheme member stops doing a regulated role with the practice or leaves employment. This is recorded in the PVG register.

### 5.9 Making a referral to Disclosure Scotland

The practice has a **legal duty** to report harmful behaviour that suggests someone may be unsuitable for a regulated role. A referral **must** be made when the practice believes the person has shown harmful (or potentially harmful) behaviour **and** they:

- were dismissed as a result, or
- would or might have been dismissed but left before this could happen, or
- were permanently moved away from work with children or protected adults.

1. This applies even if the behaviour happened outside work, or the practice found out after the person left. See Disclosure Scotland examples of harmful behaviour.
2. The Practice Owner completes the referral form on the Disclosure Scotland website. **It is an offence not to make a referral within three months.**
3. A referral to Disclosure Scotland does not replace other duties. Report to the GDC, police, social work or NHS Fife as appropriate (see 12-child-and-adult-protection.md and 07-raising-concerns-whistleblowing.md).
4. Seek advice from the indemnity provider or HR adviser [name] and record decisions.
5. If the outcome is listing, the person must be removed from the regulated role. Disclosure Scotland informs other interested parties and the GDC if the person is a registrant.
6. If Disclosure Scotland tells the practice that someone in a regulated role here is listed, **remove them from that role immediately**. It is an offence not to do so.

- The Practice Manager understands the PVG process and the Disclosure Scotland Code of Practice.

- All staff complete safeguarding training (see 12-child-and-adult-protection.md). The GDC recommends CPD in safeguarding children and young people, and vulnerable adults.
