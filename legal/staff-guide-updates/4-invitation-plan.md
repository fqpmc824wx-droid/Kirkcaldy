# 4. Invitation plan for opening

*Draft, 9 Nov 2026 (updated by the Legal chat). Owner decision (9 Nov): "Be first in line" is gone; the wording everywhere is **"Register your interest"**. We still invite in **sign-up order, households together**, because it is the fairest rule.*

## The basic rule

- We invite people **in stages** as appointments become available.
- **Households stay together.** One invitation covers everyone in the household.
- A household's place is the date of its **earliest** sign-up. Signing up twice doesn't change it.
- We invite in **sign-up order** (the owner's rule). Adults and children can both join.
- **Any exception is logged** in Notes with the date, the reason and who agreed it (for example a reasonable adjustment for a disability). No other exceptions.
- People who confirmed they still want a place go before people who never replied. If a non-replier gets back in touch, they go back to their original date.
- Being in pain, or paying for private care, **never** moves anyone up.
- We never publish how many we're inviting, or when.

**What we tell the public** (website, comments, calls):

> "We invite people from our waiting list in stages as appointments become available, keeping households together. Signing up more than once won't change your place."

Don't say "in the order you joined" in public.

## Our wording: "Register your interest"

Our ads, banner and leaflet now say **"Register your interest"**. "Be first in line" is no longer used anywhere (owner, 9 Nov).
We still invite in sign-up order, households together, and log every exception with a reason and date. That keeps the list fair and easy to explain.
If the order ever changes (for example children first), tell the owner first, and update this page.

## How many to invite each week

Only invite people we can see within **1 to 3 weeks**.

> **Households to invite = free first-exam slots in the next 1-3 weeks ÷ (people per household × share who book)**

Example: 120 slots, about 2 people per household, 70% book:
120 ÷ (2 × 0.7) = **about 86 households**.

Use our real numbers from week 2 onwards. Don't overbook until we know our real no-show rate.

## The stages

| Stage | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Test week | A small group, each one logged as an exception | Test booking, forms and texts before real patients |
| Wave 1 | The first 50-100 households on the list | Check invite, booking, reminders and attendance work |
| Wave 2 onwards | Next households in order, every week | Size each wave with the formula above |

- Spread each wave's texts over 2-3 days. Phones get busy after every send.
- People who joined a long time ago: start the call with "You joined our waiting list in [month]..."
- Book whole households in back-to-back slots, by phone if the booking system can't do families.
- Anyone who asked to wait ("not until [month]") isn't invited before that month.

## After the invitation (7 days)

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | Text I1 + invitation email |
| 2 | Not booked? Caller phones |
| 4 | Text I2 with a specific time |
| 7 | Final call with a specific time |
| After | Mark "not booked". Offer once more in a later wave |

On every booking call, ask: **"Could you read the day and time back to me?"**

## Reminders and missed appointments

- At booking: text B1 + email.
- 3 days before: text B2. Phone anyone at higher risk (big family booking, booked over 3 weeks ahead, medical form not done).
- 2 days before: medical form not done? Phone.
- 1 day before: text B3.
- Missed it: text B4 the same day and offer one more time. We **never** charge for a missed first exam.
- Children not brought: tell the practice manager. They follow up.
- Freed slots: offer to the standby list **one household at a time** (text B5), never to many people at once.

## If opening slips

1. Send text **U2** to everyone that same week, with the new month and the next update date.
2. Update the website.
3. Run the **"still want your place?"** check (text R1, 2 reminders 48 hours apart, then a call) if the wait passes 6-8 weeks, and again every 8-12 weeks.
4. Never remove anyone just because they didn't reply. Note them as not replied. They keep their place date if they come back.
5. Mark people who say no as **Withdrew**, and update anyone's new phone or email.

## Opening day

- Door notice, website banner and pinned posts: **"No walk-in sign-ups. Please join our waiting list online and we'll contact you."**
- Phone message: "To join our waiting list, visit kirkcaldydental.co.uk/register. If you've been invited, please hold."
- At the first appointment, registration happens: the patient signs the NHS forms. Any old registration ends then, not before.
- After registration, our own patients with urgent problems must be seen by us in working hours. Don't send them to 111 in working hours.
