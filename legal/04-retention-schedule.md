# Retention schedule and "delete one person everywhere" checklist

**Draft – not legal advice, owner to approve**
Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic · Version 1 · 8 November 2026

Rule of thumb: keep personal data only as long as we need it, then delete it.
The waiting list ends when the practice has finished inviting everyone. After that, people who registered are patients (their record moves into Dentally), and the rest are deleted.

## Where a person's data ends up, and how long we keep it

| Place | What is there | How long we keep it | How it is deleted |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Waiting list** tab (Google Sheet) | Name, phone, email, consent, call answers | **12 months from sign-up** (matches the privacy page). If we have not opened by then, we ask the person if they want to stay on; if yes, keep for another 12 months from that date. If they register, delete 3 months later (after moving them into Dentally). If they do not reply to the invitation, delete 6 months after it. | Monthly purge (to be automated by the leaflet chat); manual until then |
| **Call log** tab | Call times and outcomes | 12 months | Monthly purge |
| **SMS log** tab | Texts sent, phone number, status | 12 months | Monthly purge |
| **Visits** tab | Page visits from QR codes | 90 days (keep counts only after that) | Monthly purge |
| **Daily log** tab | Daily totals | Keep (counts only, no names) | – |
| **Failed sign-ups** tab | Sign-ups that did not save properly | 30 days (after fixing or adding them to the list) | Weekly check |
| **Complaints** and **Requests** log tabs | Data-protection complaints and requests | 2 years after the case is closed | Yearly check |
| **Breach log** tab | Breaches and near misses | 5 years | Yearly check |
| **info@ lead alert emails** (Gmail) | Name and phone of each new lead | 90 days | Gmail label + monthly delete; empty Bin |
| **ClickSend** history | Numbers and texts sent | 12 months | Delete in ClickSend (History / Reports), or ask ClickSend support |
| **Meta Leads Center** | Lead form answers | 30 days (after they are in the sheet) | Delete leads in Meta Business Suite → Leads Center |
| **Caller phones** (alert texts) | Name and phone of a lead | 7 days at most | Callers delete alert texts weekly. No screenshots. No saving as contacts. |
| **Do-not-contact** list | Phone and/or email, date, "do not contact" – nothing else | As long as we send messages to anyone | – (this is what stops us contacting them again) |
| **Marketing consent** records | Date, wording version, how given | While we use the consent, plus 2 years | Monthly purge |

## "Delete one person everywhere" checklist

Use this when someone asks us to delete their data, or when their time is up.
**Deadline: one month** from the request (we can extend by two more months only for complex cases, and we must tell them).
**Named person responsible:** ______________________ **Backup:** ______________________

1. [ ] Log the request in the **Requests** tab (date, who, what they asked).
2. [ ] Confirm who they are (match the phone or email they signed up with).
3. [ ] **Add them to the Do-not-contact list** (phone/email only), unless they also ask us not to keep even that. Explain that without it we might contact them again by mistake.
4. [ ] Delete their row in the **Waiting list** tab (and household members if the request covers them).
5. [ ] Delete their rows in **Call log** and **SMS log**.
6. [ ] Delete their row in **Failed sign-ups** and **Visits** if present.
7. [ ] Search Gmail (info@) for their name, phone and email. Delete the alert emails. Empty Bin.
8. [ ] Delete their record in **ClickSend** (history / contacts), if any.
9. [ ] Delete their lead in **Meta Leads Center**.
10. [ ] Ask every caller to delete any alert text about them.
11. [ ] Check the sheet's version history is the only copy left (Google removes old versions over time; do not keep exports).
12. [ ] Reply to the person to confirm it is done. Close the row in the Requests tab with the date.
