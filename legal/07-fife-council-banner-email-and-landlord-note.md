## A. Email to Fife Council planning

**To:** development.central@fife.gov.uk (Fife Council Development Management, Central team – please check this address on fife.gov.uk before sending)
**From:** info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk
**Subject:** Temporary banner on railings – 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW (listed building LB36372)

> Dear Planning team,
>
> We are opening a new NHS dental practice at 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW. We understand the building is Category B listed (Historic Environment Scotland reference LB36372).
>
> Before the practice opens, we would like to put up one temporary banner on the front railings to tell local people about our waiting list.
>
> - Size: 180 cm × 60 cm
> - Material: printed PVC/mesh banner, no lighting
> - Fixing: cable ties only, onto the railings. No drilling, screws or glue, and nothing fixed to the stonework.
> - Time: from [start date] until the practice opens, and for no longer than [number] months
> - A picture of the design and a photo of where it would go are attached.
>
> Could you please tell us:
> 1. Does this banner need **advertisement consent**, or is it covered by deemed consent?
> 2. Does it need **listed building consent**, given it is fixed with cable ties only and can be removed without any mark?
> 3. Is the building in a conservation area, and does that change anything?
> 4. If consent is needed, which form should we use, and what is the fee?
>
> We will not put the banner up until we have your reply.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Owner name]
> Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic
> 23–25 Tolbooth Street, Kirkcaldy KY1 1RW
> info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk
