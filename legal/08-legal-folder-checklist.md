- [ ] **ICO registration certificate** (with registration number and renewal date)
- [ ] **DPIA**, signed
- [ ] **Retention schedule**, signed
- [ ] **Data-protection complaints procedure**, signed
- [ ] **NHS complaints procedure** (separate, for treatment complaints)
- [ ] **Processor agreements**: ClickSend DPA + UK IDTA Addendum, Google Workspace CDPA, Meta Lead Ads Terms + Data Processing Terms, Hostinger DPA (and later VoIP and Dentally)

- [ ] Privacy page – a PDF copy of each published version, with the date

## Banner and building

- [ ] **Fife Council reply** about advertisement / listed building consent
- [ ] Landlord's reply (permission, who owns the railings)

  - who approved it;
  - **a screenshot showing Meta's AI creative options switched off** (Advantage+ creative enhancements, text generation, "Test new creative features", and the AI lead agent).
- [ ] A copy of each printed version (banner, leaflet) with the print date
