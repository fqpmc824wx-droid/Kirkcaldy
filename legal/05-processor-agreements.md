# Processor agreements list

**Draft – not legal advice, owner to approve**
Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic · Version 1 · 8 November 2026

A "processor" is an outside company that handles our patients' or leads' data for us. The law says we need a written agreement with each one. For each company below: **accept the agreement, then save a PDF or screenshot (with the date) in the legal folder.**

Do this signed in as info@kirkcaldydental.co.uk. Never share passwords with anyone to do it.

## 1. ClickSend (texts) – Australia

- **What it does:** sends the instant text to the lead and the alert text to the caller.
- **Accept and save:**
  - [ ] ClickSend **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** (on ClickSend's legal / privacy pages, or ask ClickSend support for a signed copy).
  - [ ] The **UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA Addendum)** to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. This is needed because Australia does not have a UK "adequacy" decision. If it is not already part of the DPA, ask ClickSend support for it in writing.
  - [ ] A short note in the folder: "Transfer risk check – ClickSend, Australia: only name, phone and message text are sent; data is encrypted in transit; ClickSend is bound by the Australian Privacy Act. Risk: low." (Date and initial it.)
- **Settings to check:** do not upload the waiting list to ClickSend; use a sub-account key for the scripts.

## 2. Google Workspace (sheet, email, Drive)

- **What it does:** stores the waiting-list sheet, the info@ mailbox and these documents.
- **Accept and save:**
  - [ ] **Cloud Data Processing Addendum (CDPA)**. In the Google Admin console: Account → Account settings → Legal and compliance → Data Processing Amendment / Addendum. Review and accept.
  - [ ] Add the practice's contact details for data protection in the same place.
  - [ ] Screenshot showing "Accepted" and the date.
- **Note:** this only covers Google **Workspace** accounts (like info@), not personal Gmail accounts. Staff must never use personal Gmail for lead data.

## 3. Meta (Facebook / Instagram lead ads)

- **What it does:** collects sign-ups through lead forms and holds them in the Leads Center.
- **Accept and save:**
  - [ ] **Lead Ads Terms** (accepted the first time a lead form is created; can be viewed at facebook.com/ads/leadgen/tos). Screenshot of the accepted page.
  - [ ] **Meta Data Processing Terms** (Meta acts as our processor for lead form data). Save a PDF of the current terms page with the date.
  - [ ] If the Meta Pixel is ever used on the website: the **Meta Business Tools Terms** too, and the pixel must only load after the visitor agrees to cookies.
- **Settings to check:** delete leads from Leads Center within 30 days; do not upload the list as a Custom Audience without legal advice.

## 4. Hostinger (website)

- **What it does:** hosts kirkcaldydental.co.uk. The sign-up form sends data to our Google Sheet, so Hostinger mainly holds server logs.
- **Accept and save:**
  - [ ] Hostinger **Data Processing Agreement** (part of Hostinger's terms; on its legal pages). Save a PDF with the date.
- **Note:** Hostinger is based in the EU (Lithuania), which the UK treats as adequate, so no extra transfer addendum is needed.
