# Data-protection complaints procedure

**Draft – not legal advice, owner to approve**
Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic · Version 1 · 8 November 2026

## Why we need this

Since **19 June 2026**, the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 says every organisation must have a way for people to complain about how their personal data is used. We must:
- make it easy to complain (by email and by post);
- say we have received the complaint **within 30 days**;
- look into it properly and keep the person updated;
- tell them the outcome **without undue delay**.

This covers complaints about the waiting list, texts, calls, the website and the Facebook/Instagram forms. NHS treatment complaints follow the separate NHS complaints procedure.

## Step by step

1. **Log it (same day).** Add a row to the **Complaints** tab in the waiting-list sheet:
   reference number (DP-001, DP-002…), date received, how it came in, name, what it is about, who is handling it, date acknowledged, date closed, outcome.
   Keep only what is needed. Do not copy health details into the log.
2. **Acknowledge it (within 30 days; aim for 2 working days).** Use the template below.
3. **Check who they are**, if the complaint involves their own data. Ask for no more than needed (for example, confirm the phone number they signed up with).
4. **Look into it.** Check the sheet, call log, text log and emails. If it is also a data request (for example "delete my data" or "send me my data"), follow the one-month deadline for that request too. See the "delete one person everywhere" checklist.
5. **Fix it.** Correct or delete data, stop messages, or retrain staff, as needed.
6. **Reply with the outcome (without undue delay; aim for 30 days).** Say what we found, what we did, and how to go to the ICO if they are still unhappy.
7. **Close the row** in the log with the date and outcome.
8. **If it looks like a data breach** (data sent to the wrong person, a lost phone, the sheet shared by mistake), follow the breach plan in the DPIA **straight away**. Some breaches must be reported to the ICO within 72 hours.

## Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

People can complain to the ICO, but the ICO usually expects them to complain to us first.
- Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
- Helpline: 0303 123 1113
- Post: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF

(Please check these details on ico.org.uk once a year.)
